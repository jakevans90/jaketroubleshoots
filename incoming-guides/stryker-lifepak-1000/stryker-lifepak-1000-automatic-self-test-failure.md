## What This Guide Helps With
Helps troubleshoot self-test failures related to battery, accessories, connections, and external conditions.
## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
### 1. Confirm Patient Safety and Equipment Availability
Remove the device from emergency availability if self-test failure prevents readiness confirmation. Provide a verified replacement defibrillator if needed.
Expected outcome: Patient care capability is maintained.
### 2. Confirm the Self-Test Failure Condition
Verify the reported failure and document the readiness indicator or device status.
Expected outcome: Specific failure condition is confirmed.
### 3. Check Battery Condition
Verify battery installation and condition. Replace with a known-good compatible battery if available.
Expected outcome: Self-test completes if battery condition caused the failure.
### 4. Inspect Electrodes and External Accessories
Verify electrodes and accessories are properly installed and not expired, damaged, or improperly connected.
Expected outcome: External accessory issues are corrected.
### 5. Inspect Device Condition
Check for physical damage, contamination, or environmental exposure.
Expected outcome: External causes are ruled out.
### 6. Recheck Device Readiness
Allow the device to complete normal readiness checks and verify status.
Expected outcome: Device passes self-test and indicates ready status. Troubleshooting can stop when readiness is restored.
### 7. Escalate Continued Self-Test Failure
If self-test failure remains after external checks, remove the device from service.
Expected outcome: Device is routed for qualified evaluation.
## If the Problem Persists
Common external causes have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using appropriate manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment
- Repaired or configured only by qualified personnel
Return-to-service testing should confirm defibrillator readiness before clinical use.
## Clinical Use Tip
Do not place a defibrillator into emergency service if readiness status cannot be verified.
