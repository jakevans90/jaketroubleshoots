## What This Guide Helps With
Helps troubleshoot electrode recognition problems caused by pads, connectors, placement, or accessory issues.
## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
### 1. Confirm Patient Safety and Equipment Availability
Use an alternate verified defibrillator if patient care requires immediate readiness.
Expected outcome: Patient care is not interrupted.
### 2. Confirm the Electrode Recognition Issue
Verify the reported prompt and determine whether electrodes are missing, not recognized, or intermittently detected.
Expected outcome: Exact symptom is documented.
### 3. Inspect Electrode Pads
Verify electrodes are properly installed, compatible, within usable condition, and not damaged.
Expected outcome: Electrode condition is acceptable.
### 4. Inspect Electrode Connector
Disconnect and reconnect the electrode connector. Inspect for visible damage, contamination, or poor connection.
Expected outcome: Device recognizes electrodes if connection was the cause.
### 5. Substitute Known-Good Electrodes
Install verified compatible electrodes if available.
Expected outcome: Device recognizes replacement electrodes, indicating original electrodes were the cause.
### 6. Verify Normal Operation
Confirm electrode recognition and normal device readiness.
Expected outcome: Electrode detection is restored and troubleshooting can stop.
### 7. Escalate Continued Recognition Failure
Remove the device from service if electrode recognition fails with verified accessories.
Expected outcome: Device proceeds to qualified service evaluation.
## If the Problem Persists
Common external causes such as electrode condition, connector seating, and accessory issues have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using appropriate manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment
- Repaired or configured only by qualified personnel
## Clinical Use Tip
Always verify electrode readiness before returning a defibrillator to emergency response locations.
