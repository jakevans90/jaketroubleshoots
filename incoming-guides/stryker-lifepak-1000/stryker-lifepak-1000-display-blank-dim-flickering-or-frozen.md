## What This Guide Helps With
Helps troubleshoot display problems caused by power, battery, environmental, or external operating issues.
## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
### 1. Confirm Patient Safety and Equipment Availability
If the device is needed for emergency care, replace it with a verified defibrillator before troubleshooting.
Expected outcome: Patient care capability is maintained.
### 2. Confirm Display Problem
Verify whether the display is blank, dim, flickering, frozen, or intermittently operating.
Expected outcome: Display behavior is documented.
### 3. Check Battery and Power Condition
Verify battery installation and condition. Use a known-good battery if available.
Expected outcome: Display operation improves if power condition caused the issue.
### 4. Perform Basic Restart Verification
Power the device off and back on using normal controls. Observe startup behavior.
Expected outcome: Display returns to normal operation if caused by a temporary operating condition.
### 5. Inspect External Condition
Check for impact damage, liquid exposure, contamination, or environmental conditions that may affect operation.
Expected outcome: External causes are ruled out.
### 6. Verify Functional Operation
Confirm normal display operation and readiness status.
Expected outcome: Display operates correctly and troubleshooting can stop.
### 7. Escalate Continued Display Failure
Remove the device from service if the display remains unreliable.
Expected outcome: Device proceeds to qualified evaluation.
## If the Problem Persists
Common external causes including battery condition, power issues, restart conditions, and environmental factors have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using appropriate manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment
- Repaired or configured only by qualified personnel
Verify complete device operation before returning to service.
## Clinical Use Tip
A defibrillator with an unreliable display should not remain in emergency service because readiness information may be unavailable.
