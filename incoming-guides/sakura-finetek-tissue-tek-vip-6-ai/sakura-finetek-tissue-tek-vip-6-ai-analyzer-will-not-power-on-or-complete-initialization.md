## What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshoots no-power, incomplete startup, initialization failures, external power problems, connection issues, and conditions preventing the tissue processor from becoming ready.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Protect Specimens and Maintain Workflow Continuity
Determine whether specimens are currently inside the processor or whether an active processing cycle was interrupted. If specimen integrity could be affected, immediately involve laboratory staff so specimens can be managed according to laboratory procedures.

Do not repeatedly cycle power on equipment containing specimens without considering the effect on the active process.

**Expected outcome:** Specimens are protected, laboratory staff understand the equipment status, and troubleshooting can proceed safely.

### 2. Confirm the Exact Startup Complaint
Ask staff whether the Tissue-Tek VIP 6 AI is completely unresponsive, powers on but stops during initialization, displays a message, repeatedly restarts, or remains in a not-ready condition.

Record any displayed message exactly before resetting or restarting the unit.

**Expected outcome:** The failure point is clearly identified and any useful displayed information is preserved.

### 3. Inspect for Unsafe Conditions
Check for liquid leakage, chemical odor beyond normal operating conditions, smoke, overheating, damaged wiring, evidence of fluid intrusion, or other physical damage.

If any unsafe condition is present, disconnect the equipment from use when safe to do so and stop troubleshooting.

**Expected outcome:** No condition is present that makes continued external troubleshooting unsafe. If one is found, remove the processor from service and escalate.

### 4. Verify AC Power
Confirm that the processor is connected securely to its intended power source. Inspect the accessible power cord, plug, strain relief, and external connection for damage or looseness.

Verify the outlet using an appropriate electrical test method or known-good equipment when permitted by facility policy. Check whether a facility breaker, emergency-power condition, switched outlet, or local power interruption explains the complaint.

**Expected outcome:** Stable facility power is available and the external power connection is secure. If correcting the power source restores normal startup, troubleshooting can stop after functional verification.

### 5. Check Accessible Power Controls
Verify that accessible main power controls are in the proper operating position and have not been inadvertently switched off.

Do not bypass interlocks, protective devices, or internal power circuitry.

**Expected outcome:** External power controls are correctly positioned and the unit responds normally.

### 6. Inspect External Connections
Check accessible communication, peripheral, and power connections for loose connectors, damage, pin deformation, contamination, or tension on cables.

Reseat only connections intended for normal external access.

**Expected outcome:** Required external connections are secure and undamaged. If initialization completes after correcting a loose connection, proceed to final verification.

### 7. Allow a Normal Initialization Attempt
If no unsafe condition is present and laboratory staff confirm that restarting will not jeopardize specimens, perform one controlled restart using the normal operating controls.

Observe where startup stops and whether the same condition repeats.

**Expected outcome:** The Tissue-Tek VIP 6 AI completes initialization and reaches its normal ready state. If it does, troubleshooting can stop after verifying normal operation.

### 8. Evaluate Environmental and Utility Conditions
Confirm that the unit is not being affected by obvious environmental problems such as excessive heat, blocked external ventilation, nearby fluid spills, unstable electrical service, or recent facility work.

If the problem began after a power event, relocation, maintenance activity, or facility utility change, document that relationship.

**Expected outcome:** No external environmental or utility condition is preventing initialization.

### 9. Perform Final Functional Verification
After a successful correction, verify that the system completes startup without abnormal messages, reaches its expected ready condition, and that accessible controls and indicators operate normally.

Coordinate with laboratory staff before releasing the processor for specimen processing.

**Expected outcome:** The processor initializes normally and is ready for clinical laboratory use. Troubleshooting can stop.

## If the Problem Persists

If the Tissue-Tek VIP 6 AI still will not power on or complete initialization after facility power, accessible controls, connections, environmental conditions, and a controlled restart have been checked, common external causes have been ruled out.

The remaining cause may involve internal power distribution, startup control, internal communications, sensors, safety circuits, software, or another service-level condition.

The device should be:

- Removed from service.
- Labeled Out of Service.
- Sent for repair or qualified bench/service evaluation.
- Evaluated using appropriate Sakura Finetek documentation and approved test equipment.
- Repaired or configured only by qualified personnel.

Do not proceed into internal power supplies, circuit boards, wiring assemblies, or restricted service functions without appropriate authorization and documentation.

After repair, complete applicable operational and safety verification before return to service. Knowing when to stop external troubleshooting is part of proper Clinical Engineering troubleshooting.

## Clinical Use Tip

If specimens are inside the processor during a startup failure, specimen preservation takes priority over equipment troubleshooting; coordinate immediately with laboratory personnel.

Protect specimens first, verify power and external conditions before assuming an internal failure, confirm normal startup after any correction, escalate appropriately when the fault persists, and document the complaint, cause, and resolution clearly.
