## What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshoots no-power, startup, or initialization problems caused by AC power, the external power supply, connections, accessories, or environmental conditions.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Protect the Patient and Maintain Therapy
Do not troubleshoot a DreamStation 2 while a patient depends on it for prescribed therapy. Transfer the patient to another verified therapy device or follow the clinical contingency plan before removing the unit from use.

Inspect for liquid intrusion, overheating, unusual odor, visible damage, or damaged electrical components. Remove the device from service immediately if any unsafe condition is present.

**Expected outcome:** The patient has appropriate alternate therapy and the device can be evaluated safely.

### 2. Confirm the Exact Reported Condition
Ask whether the unit was completely dead, powered off intermittently, displayed a startup message, restarted repeatedly, or stopped during therapy. Attempt normal startup while observing the display and indicators.

Record any displayed message exactly as shown.

**Expected outcome:** The reported failure is reproduced or the device starts normally. If repeated starts are normal and the original condition cannot be reproduced, continue verification before returning it to service.

### 3. Verify the AC Power Source
Confirm the wall receptacle is functional using approved test equipment or another appropriate method. Avoid relying solely on the fact that another outlet in the room works.

Check that switched receptacles, power strips, extension devices, or loose receptacle contacts are not contributing to the problem.

**Expected outcome:** Stable AC power is available at the device location. If correcting the AC source restores reliable startup, verify operation and troubleshooting can stop.

### 4. Inspect the External Power Supply and Connections
Inspect the Philips-approved power supply, AC cord, connector, and device power inlet for looseness, contamination, bent contacts, strain damage, overheating, or other physical damage.

Reseat all user-accessible power connections securely.

**Expected outcome:** Power connections are clean, undamaged, and secure. If reseating or replacing a damaged approved external power component restores normal operation, proceed to final verification.

### 5. Isolate External Accessories
Disconnect nonessential external accessories and evaluate the device in its simplest approved configuration. Do not open the enclosure or bypass protective circuits.

If another compatible approved power supply is available, use a known-good substitution to isolate the external supply from the device.

**Expected outcome:** The unit starts normally with known-good external components, identifying an external accessory or power-supply problem. Replace the defective external component and stop troubleshooting after verification.

### 6. Check Environmental and Physical Conditions
Verify that vents are unobstructed and the unit has not been exposed to excessive moisture, contamination, or obvious environmental conditions that could interfere with operation.

Allow a device moved between significantly different environments to stabilize before testing if condensation is possible.

**Expected outcome:** No environmental or physical condition is preventing normal startup.

### 7. Verify Startup and Basic Operation
Reconnect only required approved components and start the device normally. Confirm the display initializes, controls respond, therapy can be started and stopped appropriately, and no startup fault remains.

**Expected outcome:** The DreamStation 2 starts consistently and operates normally. If so, troubleshooting can stop.

### 8. Escalate an Unresolved Startup Failure
If known-good AC power, the external power supply, connections, accessories, and environment have been ruled out but the unit still does not power up or initialize reliably, stop external troubleshooting.

**Expected outcome:** The device remains out of clinical use and is escalated for qualified bench evaluation.

## If the Problem Persists

Common external power and connection causes have been ruled out. A persistent failure may involve the internal power path, control electronics, software, display subsystem, or another service-level condition.

Remove the DreamStation 2 from service, label it **Out of Service**, and send it for repair or bench evaluation. Evaluate it using appropriate Philips service documentation and approved test equipment. Internal repair, software recovery, or configuration changes should be performed only by qualified personnel.

After corrective work, complete appropriate functional and safety verification before return to service. Knowing when to stop external troubleshooting is proper troubleshooting.

## Clinical Use Tip

Maintain prescribed respiratory therapy with another verified device before troubleshooting a DreamStation 2 that will not start reliably.

## Final Thought

Protect the patient first, then work from the AC source toward the device while verifying each external component before suspecting an internal failure. Escalate unreliable equipment appropriately and document the complaint, confirmed cause, correction, and final verification clearly.

That is successful troubleshooting.
