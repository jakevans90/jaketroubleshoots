## What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshoots battery-related complaints by distinguishing the DreamStation 2, its external power arrangement, and any approved external battery or backup-power accessory.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Protect the Patient and Clarify the Power Arrangement
Do not interrupt prescribed therapy to investigate backup-power performance. Move the patient to a verified power source or alternate therapy device first.

Confirm exactly what staff are calling the “battery.” The DreamStation 2 normally depends on external electrical power; battery operation may involve a separate approved backup-power solution rather than an internal rechargeable therapy battery.

**Expected outcome:** The actual power accessory involved is identified and the patient is not dependent on equipment with uncertain runtime.

### 2. Confirm the Complaint
Determine whether the complaint is that an external battery will not charge, the device will not operate from the battery source, or runtime is shorter than expected.

Record the battery or backup-power accessory identification and any status indicators or messages.

**Expected outcome:** The reported battery problem is clearly defined rather than assuming a failure inside the DreamStation 2.

### 3. Verify Normal AC Operation
Connect the DreamStation 2 to its approved AC power supply and verify normal startup and therapy operation.

**Expected outcome:** The DreamStation 2 operates normally from AC. If it does not, troubleshoot the primary power problem before evaluating the battery accessory.

### 4. Inspect the Battery or Backup-Power Accessory
Inspect the external battery, charger, adapter, cables, and connectors for damage, loose connections, contamination, swelling, overheating, or other abnormal conditions.

Do not use a visibly damaged, swollen, leaking, or overheated battery.

**Expected outcome:** External battery components are physically safe and correctly connected.

### 5. Verify the Correct Approved Power Components
Confirm that the battery system, cable, adapter, and charger are approved and appropriate for the DreamStation 2 configuration. Do not substitute an unverified power source simply because its connector fits.

**Expected outcome:** The complete backup-power configuration is compatible and appropriate for the device.

### 6. Verify Charging Source and Indication
If the external battery system is rechargeable, verify its charger receives AC power and observe its normal charging/status indications according to the accessory manufacturer's instructions.

If practical, test the charger or battery with a known-good compatible component.

**Expected outcome:** The battery accessory accepts charging normally. If a known-good charger or cable corrects the problem, replace the defective external component and troubleshooting can stop after verification.

### 7. Evaluate Runtime Under Controlled Conditions
After the battery is appropriately charged, operate the DreamStation 2 from the approved battery system in a controlled non-patient test. Observe for unexpected shutdowns, unstable power, or abnormal battery indication.

Runtime can vary with battery condition and device configuration, so compare results with the applicable approved battery documentation rather than assuming a fixed runtime.

**Expected outcome:** The approved battery system supports stable device operation for an appropriate period based on its condition and documented capability.

### 8. Escalate Unresolved Battery Problems
If the DreamStation 2 functions normally on AC but the approved battery system repeatedly fails to charge or provide expected service, remove the questionable battery accessory from use and evaluate it separately.

If the DreamStation 2 also shows unstable operation with known-good approved power sources, remove the device from service.

**Expected outcome:** Unreliable power equipment is prevented from returning to patient use until evaluated.

## If the Problem Persists

Common external power, charger, cable, and connection causes have been ruled out. The remaining problem may involve battery aging, an external charger or power accessory, compatibility, or a device-side service-level power issue.

Remove the affected equipment from service, label it **Out of Service**, and send it for repair or bench evaluation. Use appropriate Philips and accessory-manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment. Replace or repair battery components only through qualified personnel and approved processes.

Verify stable AC and, where applicable, approved backup-power operation before return to service. Knowing when to stop external troubleshooting is proper troubleshooting.

## Clinical Use Tip

Do not rely on uncertain battery runtime for prescribed respiratory therapy; establish dependable AC power or another verified therapy device first.

## Final Thought

Separate the DreamStation 2 from the external battery system logically, protect continuity of therapy, and verify each power component before attributing the problem to the device itself. Escalate unsafe or unreliable batteries and document the verified findings clearly.

That is successful troubleshooting.
