## What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshoots no-power, incomplete startup, or initialization problems caused by external power, connections, accessories, environmental conditions, or recoverable startup conditions.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Protect Patient Testing and Confirm the Failure

Do not rely on the ACL TOP 750 LAS for patient testing while power or initialization is unreliable. Redirect coagulation testing to another verified analyzer according to laboratory procedures.

Confirm exactly what staff observed:
- No power or indicators
- Power present but display does not start
- Startup begins but stops
- Analyzer remains unavailable after initialization
- A visible message or alarm occurs

Record any displayed message exactly rather than interpreting it.

**Expected outcome:** The failure mode is clearly identified and patient testing is maintained elsewhere. If the analyzer subsequently completes startup normally and passes required verification, troubleshooting can stop.

### 2. Inspect for Immediate Safety Concerns

Check externally for:
- Liquid leakage or spills
- Burnt odor, smoke, or unusual heat
- Damaged power cord or plug
- Loose panels or obvious physical damage
- Abnormal mechanical noise during startup

Do not continue operating equipment with signs of electrical, fluid, or mechanical damage.

**Expected outcome:** No visible safety hazard is present. If damage, leakage, smoke, overheating, or burning odor is found, remove the analyzer from service and stop troubleshooting.

### 3. Verify AC Power

Confirm that the analyzer power cord is fully seated at both the analyzer and facility power source where accessible.

Verify the receptacle or approved power distribution source using an appropriate method. Check whether nearby equipment on the same circuit has also lost power.

Do not repeatedly cycle power if facility power is unstable.

**Expected outcome:** Stable facility power is available and the analyzer is securely connected. If restoring an external power connection resolves the issue and startup completes normally, troubleshooting can stop after functional verification.

### 4. Check External Power-Path Components

Inspect accessible external items such as:
- Power cord
- Approved power strip or UPS, if used
- External disconnect or power control
- Cable strain relief and plug condition

If an approved UPS is part of the installation, confirm it is operating normally and is not displaying a fault.

**Expected outcome:** The external power path is intact. If replacing or bypassing a failed approved external power component according to facility policy restores reliable operation, proceed to final verification.

### 5. Verify Startup Conditions

Confirm that covers, doors, sample-loading areas, racks, and other externally accessible components are in their normal operating positions.

Remove anything visibly obstructing normal movement or preventing a cover or loading area from seating correctly. Do not reach into moving mechanisms.

**Expected outcome:** No external obstruction or improperly positioned component prevents initialization. If startup now completes normally, troubleshooting can stop after verification.

### 6. Observe the Initialization Sequence

With the analyzer safe to operate, initiate a normal startup using approved operating controls.

Observe:
- Whether the display becomes active
- Whether startup progresses normally
- Where the sequence stops
- Whether the same message repeats
- Whether unusual sound or motion occurs

Do not enter restricted service menus or bypass interlocks.

**Expected outcome:** Initialization completes and the analyzer reaches its normal ready state. If initialization consistently stops at the same point, continue to escalation.

### 7. Check the Environment

Confirm the analyzer has adequate ventilation and that vents are not externally blocked.

Look for obvious environmental contributors such as:
- Excessive heat
- Nearby liquid exposure
- Heavy dust accumulation
- Recent relocation
- Facility power work
- Recent network or infrastructure changes occurring at the same time

**Expected outcome:** The environment is appropriate for operation and no obvious external condition explains the startup failure.

### 8. Perform Final Functional Verification

After correcting an external cause, allow the analyzer to complete normal initialization.

Verify:
- No unresolved startup alarms
- Normal user interface operation
- Required analyzer readiness checks complete
- Required laboratory QC or other release checks are completed before patient testing

**Expected outcome:** The analyzer remains stable and satisfies required return-to-service checks. Troubleshooting can stop and the analyzer may be returned to service according to laboratory policy.

## If the Problem Persists

If power and external startup conditions are verified but the ACL TOP 750 LAS still will not power on or complete initialization, common external causes have been ruled out.

The remaining cause may involve an internal power subsystem, controller, sensor, motion system, fluidics initialization condition, software state, or other service-level fault.

The analyzer should be:
- Removed from service
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation as appropriate
- Evaluated using current Werfen service documentation and approved test equipment
- Repaired or configured only by qualified personnel

Do not proceed into internal power supplies, boards, protected mechanisms, or unauthorized service functions without proper training and documentation.

Return the analyzer to clinical use only after successful startup, required functional checks, and laboratory-required QC or release testing.

Knowing when to stop external troubleshooting is proper troubleshooting.

## Clinical Use Tip

Maintain coagulation testing on another verified analyzer until the ACL TOP 750 LAS has completed startup and all required laboratory release checks.

Protect patient testing first, verify external power and startup conditions before assuming an internal failure, escalate appropriately when initialization remains abnormal, and document the confirmed complaint, cause, and resolution clearly.
