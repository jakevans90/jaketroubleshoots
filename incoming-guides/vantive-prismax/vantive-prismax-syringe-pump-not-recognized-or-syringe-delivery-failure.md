## What This Guide Helps With

Helps troubleshoot syringe pump issues related to syringe installation, connections, settings, or external setup.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Confirm patient safety
Ensure patient therapy and medication delivery needs are addressed before troubleshooting.
**Expected outcome:** Patient safety is maintained.
### 2. Confirm the syringe pump issue
Verify whether the issue is recognition, delivery, or interruption.
**Expected outcome:** Correct condition is identified.
### 3. Inspect syringe installation and external components
Verify the syringe is properly installed and visible external components are correctly positioned.
**Expected outcome:** Syringe setup is correct.
### 4. Inspect connections and tubing
Check visible syringe tubing and connections for restrictions, disconnections, or damage.
**Expected outcome:** External delivery path is intact.
### 5. Verify configuration and settings
Confirm the appropriate syringe-related settings and selections are present according to approved clinical use.
**Expected outcome:** Configuration matches intended operation.
### 6. Perform final verification
Verify syringe recognition and delivery operation.
**Expected outcome:** Syringe function is restored and troubleshooting can stop.

## If the Problem Persists

External setup, connections, and configuration issues have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using approved documentation and test equipment
- Repaired only by qualified personnel

## Clinical Use Tip

Verify alternate medication delivery if syringe delivery cannot be confirmed.
