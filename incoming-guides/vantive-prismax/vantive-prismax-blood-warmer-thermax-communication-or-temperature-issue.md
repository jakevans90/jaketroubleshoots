## What This Guide Helps With

Helps troubleshoot TherMax blood warmer issues involving connections, temperature conditions, or communication.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Confirm patient safety
Verify appropriate patient warming support and treatment continuity before troubleshooting.
**Expected outcome:** Patient safety is maintained.
### 2. Confirm the reported condition
Identify whether the issue involves communication, temperature indication, or warmer operation.
**Expected outcome:** Alarm condition is confirmed.
### 3. Inspect external connections
Check the blood warmer connection and visible cables for secure attachment and damage.
**Expected outcome:** Connections are secure.
### 4. Verify external setup and positioning
Confirm warmer components are positioned correctly and free from external interference.
**Expected outcome:** Setup is appropriate.
### 5. Compare with known-good accessories when available
Use approved compatible accessories to determine whether the issue follows an external component.
**Expected outcome:** External accessory causes are identified or ruled out.
### 6. Perform final functional verification
Verify communication and temperature operation after correction.
**Expected outcome:** Warmer function is restored and troubleshooting can stop.

## If the Problem Persists

External connections and setup issues have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service if safe operation cannot be maintained
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using appropriate manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment
- Repaired only by qualified personnel

## Clinical Use Tip

Provide alternate warming support if required before removing equipment from service.
