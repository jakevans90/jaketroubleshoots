## What This Guide Helps With

Helps troubleshoot fluid scale errors caused by bag placement, connections, environmental interference, or external setup.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Confirm patient safety
Ensure treatment status is understood and clinical staff are involved before troubleshooting.
**Expected outcome:** Patient care remains protected.
### 2. Confirm the reported scale error
Verify the exact alarm and determine which fluid scale is affected.
**Expected outcome:** Correct alarm condition is identified.
### 3. Inspect fluid bag placement
Verify the fluid container is properly positioned and not contacting other equipment or surfaces.
**Expected outcome:** Bag placement is correct.
### 4. Inspect tubing and connections
Check visible tubing for restrictions, tension, or improper routing.
**Expected outcome:** External tubing issues are corrected.
### 5. Check the surrounding environment
Verify nothing is interfering with the scale area or fluid bag movement.
**Expected outcome:** Scale area is unobstructed.
### 6. Perform final verification
Confirm the fluid scale operates normally after corrections.
**Expected outcome:** Scale error is resolved and troubleshooting can stop.

## If the Problem Persists

External causes have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using approved documentation and test equipment
- Repaired only by qualified personnel
Possible causes may involve internal scale sensing or control systems.

## Clinical Use Tip

Verify replacement therapy continuity before removing a CRRT device from patient care.
