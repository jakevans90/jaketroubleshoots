## What This Guide Helps With

Helps troubleshoot fluid balance alarms related to bags, scales, positioning, connections, or external setup.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Protect patient safety and treatment continuity
Confirm clinical staff are aware of the alarm and that patient therapy is appropriately managed before troubleshooting.
**Expected outcome:** Patient safety is maintained.
### 2. Confirm the alarm condition
Verify the exact alarm message and identify whether it involves a fluid bag, balance system, or treatment interruption.
**Expected outcome:** The condition is clearly identified.
### 3. Inspect fluid bags and scale loading
Verify fluid bags are properly positioned, supported, and connected. Check for external interference affecting hanging or weighing conditions.
**Expected outcome:** Fluid bags are correctly positioned.
### 4. Inspect external tubing and connections
Check visible tubing for kinks, restrictions, loose connections, or improper routing.
**Expected outcome:** Tubing path is clear and connections are secure.
### 5. Verify scale area is unobstructed
Inspect the external scale areas for contact with surrounding objects, equipment, or surfaces that could affect movement or measurement.
**Expected outcome:** Scale areas are free from external interference.
### 6. Perform final functional verification
Verify the device resumes normal operation without recurring fluid balance alarms.
**Expected outcome:** Fluid balance operation is restored and troubleshooting can stop.

## If the Problem Persists

External causes have been ruled out.
The device should be:
- Removed from service if safe operation cannot be maintained
- Labeled Out of Service
- Sent for repair or bench evaluation
- Evaluated using appropriate manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment
- Repaired or configured only by qualified personnel
Possible causes may involve internal scale, sensing, or control functions.

## Clinical Use Tip

Maintain therapy continuity by coordinating alternate CRRT support if the device cannot continue safely.
