## What This Guide Helps With
Troubleshooting a system that remains off, loses power during startup, or cannot reach normal operating readiness because of external power or connection problems.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
### 1. Protect the Patient and Maintain Clinical Coverage
Do not troubleshoot the Aplio i900 CV while it is required for active patient imaging. Move the examination to another verified echocardiography system when clinical imaging cannot be delayed. If there is smoke, unusual odor, excessive heat, liquid intrusion, visible electrical damage, or evidence of arcing, disconnect the system from AC power when safe and remove it from service immediately.
**Expected outcome:** Patient care is transferred safely and the system can be evaluated without affecting an active examination.

### 2. Confirm the Exact Startup Failure
Attempt normal startup while observing the system. Determine whether there are no signs of power, whether indicators illuminate without completing startup, whether the display remains blank, or whether startup repeatedly stops or restarts. Record any visible message exactly as displayed.
**Expected outcome:** The failure is narrowed to no power, incomplete startup, display-related behavior, or repeated interruption.

### 3. Verify the AC Power Source
Confirm that the system is connected securely to an appropriate facility receptacle. Inspect the accessible plug and power cord for looseness, damaged insulation, bent contacts, heat damage, or contamination. Verify the receptacle with approved test equipment or another method permitted by facility policy. Avoid relying solely on another device appearing to work from the same area.
**Expected outcome:** A reliable AC source and intact external power connection are confirmed. If correcting the outlet or power connection restores normal startup, troubleshooting can stop after final verification.

### 4. Check Accessible Power Connections and Controls
Verify that accessible system power connections are fully seated and that the normal power control operates freely. Inspect for a partially connected cord caused by transport, cleaning, or repositioning. Do not open covers or access internal power assemblies.
**Expected outcome:** External power connections and controls are secure and undamaged.

### 5. Remove Nonessential External Accessories
Disconnect nonessential external USB devices, network peripherals, removable accessories, and other externally connected equipment when doing so is safe and permitted. Leave only the equipment required for normal system startup. Attempt startup again.
**Expected outcome:** The system starts normally without an external accessory preventing initialization. If it does, isolate the accessory before returning the system to service.

### 6. Check for Environmental or Transport-Related Causes
Confirm that the system is not positioned against a wall, drape, storage item, or other obstruction that could interfere with ventilation. Check for recent transport, liquid exposure, extreme room conditions, or physical impact that may have preceded the problem.
**Expected outcome:** No obvious environmental or physical condition is preventing normal startup.

### 7. Perform a Controlled Restart When Appropriate
If the system has partial power but does not complete startup, perform only the normal shutdown/restart process available to the operator or Clinical Engineering. Do not repeatedly cycle power if abnormal noises, overheating, odor, or electrical symptoms are present.
**Expected outcome:** The Aplio i900 CV completes startup and reaches its normal ready state. If startup completes normally and remains stable, proceed to functional verification.

### 8. Perform Final Functional Verification
After successful startup, verify that the display, controls, probe recognition, basic imaging operation, and other functions necessary for clinical use operate normally. Confirm no startup warning remains. Complete any required return-to-service checks according to facility policy and approved Canon documentation.
**Expected outcome:** The system remains powered, reaches normal operating readiness, and passes applicable functional checks. Troubleshooting can stop.

### 9. Escalate an Unresolved Startup Failure
If verified AC power is present and external causes have been ruled out but the system remains dead, stalls during startup, or repeatedly restarts, stop external troubleshooting. Do not proceed into internal power supplies, computer assemblies, or board-level diagnostics unless specifically authorized and trained.
**Expected outcome:** An unresolved system-level failure is appropriately contained and referred for qualified service.

## If the Problem Persists
Common external power, connection, accessory, and environmental causes have been ruled out. The remaining problem may involve an internal power subsystem, computer hardware, startup software, internal communication, or another service-level condition.

Remove the Aplio i900 CV from service, label it Out of Service, and send it for repair or bench evaluation. Further evaluation should use appropriate Canon service documentation and approved test equipment. Internal repair, software recovery, or configuration changes should be performed only by qualified personnel.

After repair, complete appropriate functional and electrical safety testing before returning the system to clinical use. Knowing when to stop external troubleshooting and escalate is proper troubleshooting.

## Clinical Use Tip
If echocardiographic imaging is clinically time-sensitive, move the patient or examination to another verified system rather than troubleshooting during patient dependence.

## Final Thought
Protect the patient first, verify external power and connections before assuming an internal failure, confirm stable operation after correction, escalate unresolved startup problems appropriately, and document the complaint, cause, and verified resolution clearly.

That is successful troubleshooting.
