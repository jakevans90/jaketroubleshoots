## What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshoots charging failure, short mobile runtime, or unexpected shutdown caused by AC power, connections, battery condition, or charging environment.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### 1. Protect the Patient and Maintain Imaging
If the system is shutting down unexpectedly, move clinical imaging to another verified device or operate from verified AC power when appropriate before troubleshooting.

Expected outcome: Patient care does not depend on an unreliable battery-powered system.

### 2. Confirm the Battery Complaint
Determine whether the battery does not charge, charges incompletely, drains unusually quickly, or causes abrupt shutdown. Record the displayed battery condition before testing.

Expected outcome: The reported battery behavior is clearly defined.

### 3. Verify AC Power
Confirm the system is connected to its approved power source and that the power cord and accessible connections are fully seated. Test the outlet with an approved method or verify operation from a known-good outlet.

Expected outcome: Reliable AC power is available to the ultrasound system.

### 4. Inspect the Power Path
Inspect the power cord, adapter or supply if externally accessible, connectors, and strain reliefs for damage, looseness, overheating, or contamination.

Expected outcome: The external power path is intact and secure. Damaged power components are removed from service.

### 5. Confirm Charging Indication
With verified AC power connected, observe the normal battery or charging status indication.

Expected outcome: The system indicates that external power is present and battery charging is occurring. If normal charging resumes, allow appropriate charging and continue to functional verification.

### 6. Check Battery Installation and Condition
If the battery is user-accessible and intended for routine removal, verify it is correctly installed and latched. Inspect accessible areas for swelling, leakage, deformation, overheating, or damage.

Do not continue testing a visibly damaged or abnormally hot battery.

Expected outcome: The battery is correctly installed and shows no visible signs requiring immediate removal from service.

### 7. Compare With a Known-Good Battery When Approved
If facility procedures and the system design permit routine battery replacement, test with a compatible known-good battery.

Expected outcome: Normal operation with a known-good battery indicates the original battery should be replaced or evaluated according to approved procedures.

### 8. Evaluate Mobile Operation
After establishing adequate charge, disconnect AC power and observe the system during normal nonpatient operation. Check for sudden percentage changes, shutdown, or loss of power during ordinary movement.

Expected outcome: The system remains powered and battery indication changes normally. If stable, troubleshooting can stop after final verification.

### 9. Perform Final Functional Verification
Verify AC operation, charging indication, battery-powered operation, normal shutdown/startup, and mobility without interruption.

Expected outcome: Reliable operation is confirmed on both applicable power sources.

## If the Problem Persists

The outlet, external power connections, battery installation, and accessible battery condition have been checked. Persistent failure may involve a depleted or failed battery, charging circuitry, power-management electronics, internal connections, or another service-level condition.

Remove the system from service if unexpected shutdown could interrupt clinical imaging. Label it **Out of Service** and send it for repair or bench evaluation using appropriate Fujifilm Sonosite documentation and approved test equipment. Battery replacement, repair, or configuration should be performed only by qualified personnel.

Complete functional and applicable electrical-safety testing before return to service. Knowing when to stop external troubleshooting is proper troubleshooting.

## Clinical Use Tip

Do not begin a procedure that requires mobility when battery condition or remaining runtime cannot be trusted.

## Final Thought

Unexpected power loss can interrupt imaging at a critical moment. Verify the complete external power path and battery condition first, then escalate persistent charging or power-management problems with clear documentation.
