Baxter Novum IQ

Administration Set Loading Error

Asset Type

Infusion Pump

Manufacturer

Baxter

Model

Novum IQ

What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshooting administration-set loading errors caused by incorrect tubing placement, set incompatibility, closed clamps, damaged tubing, door obstruction, or loading-mechanism faults.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

1. Ensure Patient Safety First

Do not unload, reposition, or replace an administration set while the pump is actively delivering a critical infusion.

  • Notify nursing and the appropriate clinical team.
  • Transfer the infusion to another verified pump when interruption could affect the patient.
  • Clamp the administration set before opening the pump door to prevent unintended free flow.
  • Follow facility medication-handling procedures when transferring or restarting the infusion.

Expected outcome: The patient continues receiving prescribed therapy without depending on the affected pump.

2. Confirm the Exact Loading Problem

Record the complete displayed message and determine whether:

  • The pump does not recognize the administration set.
  • The door will not close or latch.
  • The slide clamp is not captured or released correctly.
  • The loading error appears immediately after closing the door.
  • The error occurs with one set or with multiple sets.

Check the event history when available.

Expected outcome: The failure is clearly identified as a set-related, loading-related, or pump-related problem.

3. Verify the Administration Set

Confirm that the tubing is a Baxter administration set approved for use with the Novum IQ large-volume pump.

Inspect the set for:

  • Incorrect set type
  • Damaged or deformed slide clamp
  • Flattened, stretched, or kinked tubing
  • Contamination or sticky residue
  • Improperly assembled components
  • Packaging damage or an expired use date

Do not force an incompatible set into the pumping channel.

Expected outcome: The correct, undamaged administration set is available. If replacing the set resolves the error, complete the required operational check and stop.

4. Fully Open the Pump Door

Remove the set safely and open the pump door completely before attempting to reload it.

Inspect the door and loading channel externally for:

  • Tubing left in the channel
  • Adhesive residue
  • Dried fluid
  • Labels or foreign material
  • A slide-clamp fragment
  • Visible damage to the door or latch

Clean only according to Baxter-approved procedures. Do not insert tools into the loading mechanism.

Expected outcome: The door opens fully and the loading channel is unobstructed.

5. Close the Downstream Roller Clamp

Before reloading the set, verify that the downstream roller clamp is completely closed.

This protects against free flow while the door is open and the set is being positioned.

Expected outcome: Fluid cannot flow freely through the unloaded administration set.

6. Position the Slide Clamp Correctly

Place the administration set’s slide clamp into the designated loading area in the proper orientation.

Confirm that:

  • The clamp is seated flat.
  • It is not upside down or angled.
  • Tubing is not wrapped around the clamp.
  • The clamp is not cracked, bent, or partially engaged.

Do not hold the clamp outside its intended position while closing the door.

Expected outcome: The slide clamp sits securely in the loading mechanism without resistance.

7. Load the Tubing Without Slack

Route the tubing through the pumping channel exactly as indicated by the pump’s loading guides.

Ensure that:

  • The tubing is straight and taut through the channel.
  • No loop or slack remains inside the pump.
  • The tubing is fully seated in each guide.
  • The tubing is not twisted, stretched, doubled, or pinched.
  • The upper and lower tubing sections exit through their intended paths.

Baxter specifically instructs users to fully open the door and load the tubing taut, without slack in the pumping channel. Improper loading may contribute to inaccurate delivery.

Expected outcome: The tubing follows the complete pumping path and remains seated when released.

8. Close the Door Normally

Close the pump door using normal hand pressure.

Do not:

  • Slam the door
  • Force the latch
  • Use tools
  • Hold the tubing while latching
  • Bypass the slide-clamp mechanism

If the door requires excessive force, reopen it and inspect the set position again.

Expected outcome: The door closes and latches smoothly. The pump recognizes the set without displaying a loading error.

9. Confirm Slide-Clamp Release

After the door is closed, verify that the slide clamp is released or ejected as designed.

Then fully open the downstream roller clamp before beginning the infusion.

If the clamp remains trapped, does not release normally, or appears damaged, do not start the infusion.

Expected outcome: The set is secured by the pump, the slide clamp operates normally, and the downstream tubing is unobstructed.

10. Complete the Pump’s Setup Checks

Before clinical use:

  • Confirm that no loading or door alarm remains.
  • Verify the programmed medication, concentration, rate, and volume.
  • Inspect for visible drops or flow where clinically appropriate.
  • Confirm that the tubing is not kinked upstream or downstream.
  • Check that the pump passes its normal startup and infusion checks.
  • Observe the beginning of delivery for unexpected alarms or flow behavior.

Expected outcome: The pump begins delivery normally and displays the expected infusion status.

If the loading error is resolved, remove any test supplies, clean the device as required, document the work, and return it to service only after verification.

11. Test With a New Compatible Set

If the error remains, repeat the loading process using a new, known-good Baxter-compatible administration set.

Do not repeatedly reload tubing that has become crushed, stretched, or visibly deformed.

If the new set loads successfully, remove the original set from use and report recurring lot-specific problems through facility procedures.

If multiple known-good sets produce the same error, suspect a door, sensor, slide-clamp, or loading-mechanism problem.

Expected outcome: The test distinguishes a defective administration set from a pump-related failure.

12. Compare With a Known-Good Pump

When available, load a new compatible set into another verified Novum IQ pump.

If the same set type loads normally in the comparison pump, the original pump likely requires service.

If several sets from the same lot fail in multiple pumps, quarantine the affected sets and notify Supply Chain, Pharmacy, Risk Management, or Baxter as required.

Do not use patient medication or connect a patient during this comparison.

Expected outcome: The problem is isolated to the pump, administration set, or set inventory.

If the Problem Persists

If multiple compatible, undamaged administration sets are loaded correctly but the error remains, common external causes have been ruled out. The door latch, set-detection system, slide-clamp mechanism, tubing channel, or another internal component may require service.

The device should be:

  • Removed from service
  • Labeled Out of Service
  • Sent for repair or bench evaluation

Do not defeat sensors, force the door, lubricate the mechanism, or disassemble the pump outside an authorized service procedure.

Knowing when to stop and escalate is proper troubleshooting.

Clinical Use Tip

Never correct a set-loading problem while a patient depends on an uninterrupted infusion. Transfer critical therapy first, close the roller clamp before unloading, and verify the complete infusion setup after reloading.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"Nursing reported that the Baxter Novum IQ displayed an administration-set loading error and would not begin the infusion."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"The administration-set tubing was loaded with slack in the pumping channel, preventing proper set recognition when the door was closed."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed the set using free-flow precautions, reloaded new compatible tubing taut through the channel, verified slide-clamp operation, and completed a successful functional infusion test."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Exact loading-error message
  • Pump serial number and software version
  • Administration-set product and lot number
  • Door fully opened before loading
  • Roller clamp closed before unloading
  • Slide-clamp condition and operation
  • Tubing position or visible slack
  • Known-good set tested
  • Comparison pump tested
  • Door or latch condition
  • Evidence of contamination or fluid intrusion
  • Alarm and event-history findings
  • Final device status

Final Thought

Safe troubleshooting begins by protecting the patient and preventing free flow. Check set compatibility, clamp position, tubing routing, and door operation before suspecting an internal failure. Clear CCR documentation also helps identify recurring pump or administration-set trends.

That is successful troubleshooting.
