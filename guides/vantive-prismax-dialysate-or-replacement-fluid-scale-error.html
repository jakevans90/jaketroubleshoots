Dialysate or Replacement Fluid Scale Error

Asset Type

Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT)

Manufacturer

Vantive

Model

PrisMax

What This Guide Helps With

Helps troubleshoot fluid scale errors caused by bag placement, connections, environmental interference, or external setup.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

1. Confirm patient safety

Ensure treatment status is understood and clinical staff are involved before troubleshooting. Expected outcome: Patient care remains protected.

2. Confirm the reported scale error

Verify the exact alarm and determine which fluid scale is affected. Expected outcome: Correct alarm condition is identified.

3. Inspect fluid bag placement

Verify the fluid container is properly positioned and not contacting other equipment or surfaces. Expected outcome: Bag placement is correct.

4. Inspect tubing and connections

Check visible tubing for restrictions, tension, or improper routing. Expected outcome: External tubing issues are corrected.

5. Check the surrounding environment

Verify nothing is interfering with the scale area or fluid bag movement. Expected outcome: Scale area is unobstructed.

6. Perform final verification

Confirm the fluid scale operates normally after corrections. Expected outcome: Scale error is resolved and troubleshooting can stop.

If the Problem Persists

External causes have been ruled out. The device should be:

  • Removed from service
  • Labeled Out of Service
  • Sent for repair or bench evaluation
  • Evaluated using approved documentation and test equipment
  • Repaired only by qualified personnel

Possible causes may involve internal scale sensing or control systems.

Clinical Use Tip

Verify replacement therapy continuity before removing a CRRT device from patient care.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"Clinical staff reported a dialysate scale error during PrisMax operation."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Clinical Engineering found the fluid container position was affecting external scale movement."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Clinical Engineering corrected the setup, verified proper operation, and returned the device to service."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Alarm message
  • Affected scale
  • Bag position
  • Tubing condition
  • Environmental inspection
  • Final verification

Final Thought

Logical troubleshooting starts with external causes, confirms operation, and escalates appropriately when internal evaluation is needed. Good documentation supports safe CRRT service. That is successful troubleshooting.
