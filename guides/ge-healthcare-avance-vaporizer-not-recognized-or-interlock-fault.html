GE Healthcare Avance

Vaporizer Not Recognized or Interlock Fault

Asset Type

Anesthesia Machine

Manufacturer

GE Healthcare

Model

Avance

What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshooting vaporizer mounting, recognition, locking, or interlock problems caused by positioning, compatibility, contamination, or damaged external components.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

1. Ensure Patient Safety First

Do not remove, exchange, reseat, or test a vaporizer while the Avance is actively supporting a patient.

If vaporizer operation or anesthetic-agent delivery is uncertain:

  • Notify the anesthesia provider immediately.
  • Have the provider establish an approved alternate anesthetic plan.
  • Transfer the patient to another verified anesthesia machine when necessary.
  • Maintain appropriate ventilation and independent gas monitoring.

Expected: Patient care no longer depends on equipment with uncertain anesthetic-agent delivery.

Continue troubleshooting only after the machine is removed from active clinical use.

2. Confirm the Exact Failure Condition

Review the reported problem and determine whether:

  • The vaporizer cannot be installed.
  • The locking lever will not fully engage.
  • The concentration dial will not turn.
  • Two vaporizers can be turned on simultaneously.
  • The machine does not respond as expected when the vaporizer is activated.
  • The problem occurs in one mounting position or every position.

Record the vaporizer type, anesthetic agent, mounting position, and any displayed messages.

Expected: The complaint is clearly defined before components are exchanged.

3. Verify Vaporizer Compatibility

Confirm that the installed vaporizer is an approved Selectatec-compatible vaporizer intended for use with the Avance configuration.

Do not force an incompatible vaporizer onto the manifold.

GE describes the Avance vaporization system as using a Selectatec interlocking mounting system that bypasses vaporizers not selected for use.

Expected: The vaporizer and mounting system are mechanically compatible.

If the vaporizer is not approved or compatible, remove it from service and stop troubleshooting.

4. Inspect the Vaporizer Externally

With the vaporizer removed and its concentration dial set to OFF, inspect for:

  • Cracks or impact damage
  • Bent or damaged mounting components
  • Loose locking hardware
  • Residue, liquid agent, or contamination
  • Damaged concentration dial or release mechanism
  • Missing labels or unreadable agent identification
  • Signs that the vaporizer was dropped

Do not install a vaporizer showing physical damage or leakage.

Expected: The vaporizer is clean, intact, and safe to mount.

5. Inspect the Avance Vaporizer Manifold

Examine the mounting positions without opening the machine.

Check for:

  • Debris or liquid on the mounting surface
  • Bent alignment features
  • Damaged valve pins
  • Loose manifold hardware
  • Obstructions preventing full seating
  • Visible damage around the interlock mechanism

Clean only according to facility procedures and manufacturer-approved methods. Do not lubricate the manifold or interlock.

Expected: The mounting surface is clean, unobstructed, and visibly undamaged.

6. Reseat and Lock the Vaporizer

Place the vaporizer squarely onto the mounting position without rocking or forcing it.

Turn the locking lever completely to the locked position. Confirm that:

  • The vaporizer sits flat against the manifold.
  • The vaporizer does not lift or move.
  • The locking lever reaches its full locked position.
  • The concentration dial remains at OFF during installation.

Incomplete seating may prevent proper valve engagement or interlock operation.

Expected: The vaporizer is firmly mounted and cannot be removed while locked.

If reseating resolves the issue, complete the machine checkout and stop.

7. Check for Interference From the Adjacent Vaporizer

Set both vaporizer concentration dials to OFF.

Confirm that both vaporizers are:

  • Fully seated
  • Fully locked
  • Correctly aligned
  • Not physically contacting or obstructing each other

Attempt to turn on one vaporizer at a time. The Selectatec interlock should prevent more than one mounted vaporizer from being activated simultaneously.

Expected: One vaporizer can be selected while the other remains mechanically locked at OFF.

If both vaporizers can be turned on simultaneously, immediately remove the anesthesia machine from service.

8. Isolate the Vaporizer From the Mounting Position

Using another compatible, verified vaporizer, repeat the mounting and interlock check in the same position.

Then install the original vaporizer in the alternate mounting position, when the machine configuration permits.

Interpret the results:

  • If the problem follows the vaporizer, suspect a vaporizer mechanical fault.
  • If multiple vaporizers fail in one position, suspect the Avance mounting or interlock mechanism.
  • If the fault disappears after reseating, suspect incomplete installation or contamination.

Expected: The problem is isolated to either the vaporizer or the anesthesia machine.

Do not use a patient-care vaporizer merely as an uncontrolled test component. Preserve clinical readiness.

9. Verify Vaporizer Dial Operation

With the vaporizer properly mounted and the adjacent vaporizer set to OFF:

  • Press the concentration-control release as designed.
  • Turn the dial slowly from OFF to a low concentration.
  • Confirm smooth movement without excessive force.
  • Return the dial fully to OFF.

Do not force a stuck dial. A concentration control that binds, slips, or cannot return securely to OFF requires vaporizer service.

Expected: The dial moves normally and positively returns to OFF.

10. Run the Avance Preoperative Checkout

After the vaporizer is securely installed:

  • Confirm both vaporizers are filled appropriately and initially set to OFF.
  • Run the required Avance checkout or full system test.
  • Observe for leakage-test failures, gas-delivery abnormalities, or vaporizer-related messages.
  • Verify agent output only with approved test equipment and facility procedures.

GE checkout guidance includes confirming that vaporizers are off and appropriately filled before system testing.

Expected: The machine completes its checkout with normal vaporizer and interlock operation.

If the checkout passes and the original complaint cannot be reproduced, document the findings and return the device according to facility policy.

If the Problem Persists

If compatibility, seating, mounting surfaces, adjacent-vaporizer interference, and external components have been checked, the fault may involve the vaporizer locking mechanism, Selectatec valves, interlock assembly, or internal gas-delivery components.

The device should be:

  • Removed from service
  • Labeled Out of Service
  • Sent for authorized repair or bench evaluation
  • Evaluated using the applicable GE service procedures and calibrated test equipment

A vaporizer with leakage, physical damage, abnormal dial operation, or failed interlock behavior must also be removed from service.

Do not bypass, defeat, adjust, or lubricate the interlock mechanism. Knowing when to stop is proper troubleshooting.

Clinical Use Tip

Never exchange or reseat vaporizers while the machine is delivering anesthesia. Move the patient to verified equipment and confirm alternate anesthetic delivery before testing.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"Anesthesia staff reported that the Avance vaporizer would not lock into position and the concentration dial could not be activated."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Inspection found the vaporizer incompletely seated because residue on the Selectatec mounting surface prevented full locking-lever engagement."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed the vaporizer, cleaned the external mounting surface using an approved method, reinstalled and locked the vaporizer, verified interlock operation, and completed the Avance checkout successfully."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Vaporizer manufacturer, model, and serial number
  • Anesthetic agent type
  • Left or right mounting position
  • Vaporizer compatibility verified
  • Locking-lever position
  • Physical damage or contamination found
  • Alternate vaporizer test results
  • Interlock behavior
  • Leakage or checkout-test results
  • Agent-output verification results
  • Final device and vaporizer status

Final Thought

Vaporizer and interlock faults directly affect anesthetic-agent delivery. Protect the patient first, verify mounting and compatibility logically, and escalate any unresolved mechanical or gas-path concern. Accurate CCR documentation preserves both service history and patient safety.

That is successful troubleshooting.
