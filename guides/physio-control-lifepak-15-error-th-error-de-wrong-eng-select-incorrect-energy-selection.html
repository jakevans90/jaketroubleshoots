Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15

Error: TH_ERROR_DE_WRONG_ENG_SELECT Incorrect Energy Selection

Asset Type

Defibrillator

Manufacturer

Physio-Control

Model

LIFEPAK 15

What This Guide Helps With

This guide helps troubleshoot TH_ERROR_DE_WRONG_ENG_SELECT when the Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15 reports an incorrect defibrillation energy selection condition or therapy control mismatch. This error may affect confidence in selected energy, charge behavior, therapy verification, or safe defibrillator readiness.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

Ensure Patient Safety First

  • Remove the LIFEPAK 15 from patient use immediately if this error is active, repeated, or associated with failed charging, incorrect energy selection, or therapy delivery concerns.
  • Provide clinical staff with another verified defibrillator before continuing troubleshooting.
  • Do not attempt repeated shock delivery or therapy evaluation on an active patient with a device that has reported a therapy service code.
  • Expected: Patient care is not dependent on a defibrillator with an unresolved therapy service code.

Verify the Reported Error and Behavior

  • Confirm the exact error reported is TH_ERROR_DE_WRONG_ENG_SELECT.
  • Confirm the associated therapy service code is a00a.
  • Review the service log and staff report to determine when the error occurred.
  • Check whether the error appeared during startup, energy selection, charge attempt, manual defibrillator testing, daily check, or intermittent clinical use.
  • Expected: The error is confirmed in the service log or reproduced during controlled bench evaluation.

Understand What the Error Means

  • This error indicates the LIFEPAK 15 detected an incorrect energy selection condition.
  • The reported parameter is the energy index, meaning the therapy system identified a mismatch or invalid condition related to the selected defibrillation energy level.
  • Possible real-world impact may include energy selection not being interpreted correctly, abnormal charge behavior, therapy system fault during defibrillator operation, or inability to safely verify selected energy.
  • Expected: Clinical Engineering treats this as a therapy-related service condition, not a routine user setup issue.

Check for Obvious External or User-Accessible Issues

  • Inspect the front panel controls, energy select knob or buttons, and therapy-related controls for physical damage.
  • Look for sticking or intermittent control action, liquid contamination, cracked housing, loose selector components, or evidence of impact damage.
  • Confirm the controls operate smoothly and do not show obvious contamination or mechanical failure.
  • Expected: Controls operate normally and show no obvious damage or contamination.

Verify Power Source Stability

  • Install a known-good charged battery.
  • Connect the unit to AC power if available.
  • Power cycle the device and observe whether the error returns.
  • Do not dismiss a repeated therapy error as a battery issue unless testing clearly supports that conclusion.
  • Expected: Device powers on normally without repeating the therapy service code.

Check Therapy Cable and Test Setup Before Controlled Testing

  • Inspect the therapy cable, paddles, QUIK-COMBO cable, test load, and connector area for damage, contamination, loose pins, or poor seating.
  • Confirm the therapy cable is fully seated.
  • Perform testing only with an approved defibrillator analyzer or test load.
  • Expected: Therapy accessories are intact, connected correctly, and suitable for controlled bench testing.

Attempt to Reproduce Under Controlled Bench Conditions Only

  • Do not attempt repeated therapy testing in a patient care area.
  • On the bench, observe whether the error appears when powering on the unit, changing selected energy levels, initiating charge, performing the daily check, running the user test, or connecting therapy accessories.
  • Document whether the issue appears consistently or intermittently.
  • Expected: The error is either reproducible or clearly documented from the service log.

Process the Service Log

  • Retrieve and review the LIFEPAK 15 service log according to facility procedure.
  • Confirm therapy service code a00a.
  • Document any associated therapy events, repeated occurrences, related service codes, or abnormal charge and energy selection behavior.
  • Expected: The service code is captured before clearing, repair, or return-to-service decisions.

Do Not Clear and Return Without Therapy Verification

  • If the error clears after power cycling or log processing, do not immediately return the defibrillator to service.
  • Perform appropriate LIFEPAK 15 therapy testing, including defibrillator charge and delivered-energy verification per facility and service procedures.
  • Verify that selected energy, charge behavior, and delivered energy match expected results before return to clinical use.
  • Expected: Selected energy, charge behavior, and delivered energy are verified before return to service.

Determine Whether the Issue Was External or Device-Related

  • If physical control damage, contamination, accessory damage, or setup error is found and corrected, repeat controlled therapy testing.
  • If no external cause is found, or if the service code repeats, treat the condition as an internal therapy system fault.
  • Expected: The issue is narrowed to either an external control condition or a device-level therapy fault.

Stop When Resolved

  • If the error does not return, the service log is reviewed, controls function normally, and the LIFEPAK 15 passes required defibrillator energy and therapy verification testing, the device may be returned to service.
  • Document the final status clearly in the work order.
  • Expected: The unit is returned only after error review, external checks, and therapy verification are complete.

If the Problem Persists

If TH_ERROR_DE_WRONG_ENG_SELECT or therapy service code a00a persists after external checks, power verification, accessory inspection, service log review, and controlled bench testing, remove the LIFEPAK 15 from service.

At that point, external causes such as damaged controls, unstable power, accessory issues, and test setup problems have been ruled out. Label the device Out of Service and send it for repair or bench evaluation.

Persistent incorrect energy selection errors may indicate a therapy control, processor, calibration, or internal therapy system issue that should not be handled as a simple user-correctable problem.

Clinical Use Tip

Never troubleshoot this error on an active patient. Move the patient to a backup defibrillator first and maintain therapy continuity. A defibrillator with an unresolved therapy service code should not be used for patient care, even if it powers on normally.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"LIFEPAK 15 reported TH_ERROR_DE_WRONG_ENG_SELECT with therapy service code a00a during defibrillator use or daily check."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Service log confirmed therapy code a00a related to incorrect energy selection. External power, therapy cable, test setup, and control condition were checked. No user setup issue was confirmed, and persistent code indicates likely internal therapy system fault."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed LIFEPAK 15 from service. Processed service log and performed controlled therapy evaluation. Unit held for repair or bench service and defibrillator energy and charge testing before return to service."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Exact error behavior, including TH_ERROR_DE_WRONG_ENG_SELECT and therapy service code a00a.
  • Accessories swapped, including therapy cable, paddles, QUIK-COMBO cable, analyzer, or test load.
  • Power behavior, including battery condition, AC power use, startup behavior, and whether the error repeated after power cycle.
  • Environmental factors, including liquid exposure, impact damage, storage conditions, or patient care area where the error occurred.
  • Indicator lights, service LED status, related service codes, charge behavior, and delivered-energy test results.
  • Final device status, including returned to service, held for repair, sent for bench evaluation, or labeled Out of Service.

Final Thought

Therapy-related service codes on a defibrillator require careful interpretation. Start with patient safety, confirm the error, check external conditions, and use controlled bench testing to verify therapy performance. If selected energy cannot be trusted or the service code persists, escalation is the correct repair path. Clear documentation protects the patient, the technician, and the clinical team.

That is successful troubleshooting.
