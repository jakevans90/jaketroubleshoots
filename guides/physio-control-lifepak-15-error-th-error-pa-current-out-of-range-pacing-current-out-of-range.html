Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15

Error: TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE Pacing Current Out of Range

Asset Type

Defibrillator

Manufacturer

Physio-Control

Model

LIFEPAK 15

What This Guide Helps With

This guide helps troubleshoot TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE on the Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15 when the selected pacing current does not match the actual delivered pacing output tolerance. This is a therapy-output accuracy concern and should be handled as a pacing fault, not as a routine user setup issue.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

Ensure Patient Safety First

  • Remove the LIFEPAK 15 from patient use if the error appears during operation, testing, startup, or pacing setup.
  • If pacing is clinically needed, immediately provide another verified defibrillator/monitor capable of pacing.
  • Do not troubleshoot pacing output problems while the device is connected to an active patient.
  • Expected: Patient care continues using a known-good device.

Verify the Reported Error and Behavior

  • Confirm the exact error displayed or recorded is TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE.
  • Confirm the associated therapy service code is a011.
  • Review whether the error occurs during startup, pacing setup, therapy testing, service review, or performance inspection.
  • Check whether pacing is unavailable, pacing output stops, a service indicator is present, or a therapy service code is logged.
  • Expected: The error is reproducible or clearly documented in the service log.

Translate the Error Into the Real-World Failure

  • This error means the LIFEPAK 15 detected that actual pacing current output is outside tolerance compared with the selected pacing current.
  • The error parameter may include the selected pacing current and actual measured pacing current.
  • Expected: The device is identifying a pacing output control or monitoring problem.
  • This is not a normal lead connection message or a basic operator-correctable pacing setup issue.

Check for Obvious External Issues

  • Inspect the device exterior, therapy cable, therapy connector, and pacing/defib cable connection.
  • Look for bent connector pins, damaged cable insulation, fluid contamination, cracked housing, loose connections, impact damage, or a damaged therapy pads connector.
  • Expected: No obvious cable, connector, or physical damage is found.
  • If damage is found, remove the damaged accessory or device from use and continue only with proper controlled test equipment.

Check the Therapy Cable and Accessories

  • If available, connect a known-good compatible therapy cable and appropriate test load or analyzer setup.
  • Do not test pacing output on a patient.
  • Expected: The device either continues to report the error or the issue follows a damaged accessory.
  • If a damaged or incorrect cable is found, replace the accessory and retest per facility procedure.
  • If the error clears after replacing an external accessory, return the unit only after required pacing output verification and functional checks are completed.

Verify Power Condition

  • Confirm the LIFEPAK 15 is connected to a stable power source or has a known-good charged battery installed.
  • Inspect battery contacts and confirm there are no shutdowns, resets, low-power symptoms, or unrelated power faults.
  • Expected: The device powers normally without instability.
  • A persistent pacing current tolerance error should still be treated as a device fault even if power appears normal.

Review the Service Log or Reported Service Code

  • Check the service log or reported service information for therapy service code a011.
  • Note whether the error is isolated or appears with other pacing or therapy faults.
  • Expected: The error history supports a pacing current output tolerance condition.
  • Multiple therapy-related codes may indicate a broader therapy system failure requiring repair evaluation.

Do Not Attempt Clinical Return Based Only on Restarting

  • Power cycling may temporarily clear a displayed message, but it does not prove pacing output is safe or within tolerance.
  • Keep the unit out of service until pacing output is verified with the correct test setup.
  • Expected: The unit remains out of service until pacing output accuracy is confirmed.

Determine Whether the Issue Is Accessory-Related or Device-Related

  • If the error resolves with a known-good therapy cable or accessory and the device passes required performance testing, the issue may have been accessory-related.
  • If the error remains with known-good accessories and proper test setup, treat it as device-related.
  • Expected: Persistent TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE indicates an internal pacing therapy fault.

Stop When Resolved

  • If an external cause is found, corrected, and pacing output passes required verification, document the correction and return the device to service per facility policy.
  • If the error persists, do not continue repeated bedside checks.
  • Expected: The troubleshooting path stops before unnecessary or unsafe clinical use occurs.

If the Problem Persists

If external accessories, cable condition, power condition, and setup have been ruled out, a persistent TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE with therapy service code a011 strongly indicates an internal pacing therapy failure.

The device should be removed from service, labeled Out of Service, and sent for repair or bench evaluation.

Do not return the LIFEPAK 15 to service until pacing output accuracy and therapy function are verified according to facility and manufacturer service requirements.

Clinical Use Tip

Never troubleshoot pacing output problems on an active patient. If pacing is needed and the LIFEPAK 15 reports a pacing current tolerance error, move the patient to a backup verified defibrillator/monitor first and maintain therapy continuity before equipment troubleshooting continues.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"LIFEPAK 15 reported TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE with therapy service code a011. Reported condition indicates pacing current out of tolerance between selected current and actual current."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"External cable, accessory, and power checks were performed. Error persisted with no correctable external issue found. Fault indicates likely internal pacing output tolerance failure."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed LIFEPAK 15 from service and labeled Out of Service. Sent for repair or bench evaluation. Pacing output verification required after repair before return to clinical use."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Alarm behavior, including when TH_ERROR_PA_CURRENT_OUT_OF_RANGE appeared
  • Accessories swapped, including therapy cable, pads connector, and test load setup
  • Power behavior, including battery condition, AC power condition, shutdowns, or resets
  • Environmental factors, including fluid exposure, impact damage, contamination, or storage conditions
  • Indicator lights, including Service LED or other therapy fault indicators
  • Final device status, including Out of Service status, repair disposition, and pacing output verification after repair

Final Thought

A pacing current out-of-range error on a defibrillator is a therapy accuracy concern, not a routine user issue. Clinical Engineering should confirm the error, rule out simple external causes, remove the device from service when the condition persists, and document the failure clearly. Proper escalation protects the patient and ensures the unit is not returned until pacing output is verified.

That is successful troubleshooting.
