Vantive PrisMax

Blood Warmer / TherMax Communication or Temperature Issue

Asset Type

Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT)

Manufacturer

Vantive

Model

PrisMax

What This Guide Helps With

Helps troubleshoot TherMax blood warmer issues involving connections, temperature conditions, or communication.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

1. Confirm patient safety

Verify appropriate patient warming support and treatment continuity before troubleshooting. Expected outcome: Patient safety is maintained.

2. Confirm the reported condition

Identify whether the issue involves communication, temperature indication, or warmer operation. Expected outcome: Alarm condition is confirmed.

3. Inspect external connections

Check the blood warmer connection and visible cables for secure attachment and damage. Expected outcome: Connections are secure.

4. Verify external setup and positioning

Confirm warmer components are positioned correctly and free from external interference. Expected outcome: Setup is appropriate.

5. Compare with known-good accessories when available

Use approved compatible accessories to determine whether the issue follows an external component. Expected outcome: External accessory causes are identified or ruled out.

6. Perform final functional verification

Verify communication and temperature operation after correction. Expected outcome: Warmer function is restored and troubleshooting can stop.

If the Problem Persists

External connections and setup issues have been ruled out. The device should be:

  • Removed from service if safe operation cannot be maintained
  • Labeled Out of Service
  • Sent for repair or bench evaluation
  • Evaluated using appropriate manufacturer documentation and approved test equipment
  • Repaired only by qualified personnel

Clinical Use Tip

Provide alternate warming support if required before removing equipment from service.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"Clinical staff reported a TherMax communication issue during PrisMax CRRT treatment."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Clinical Engineering found the external communication connection was not fully seated."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Clinical Engineering secured the connection, verified warmer communication, and returned the system to service."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Alarm message
  • Connection condition
  • Cable inspection
  • Temperature observations
  • Accessory used
  • Final verification

Final Thought

Safe troubleshooting requires protecting patient therapy, checking external causes first, and escalating when internal evaluation is necessary. Proper documentation supports reliable clinical equipment service. That is successful troubleshooting.
