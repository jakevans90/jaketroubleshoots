Error: PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST RAM Test Failed

What This Guide Helps With

This guide helps troubleshoot PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST, therapy service code a212, where the pacer processor RAM test fails and therapy reliability may be affected.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

Ensure Patient Safety First

  • Remove the LIFEPAK 15 from patient use immediately if this error is reported or visible.
  • Do not use the device for monitoring, defibrillation, cardioversion, or pacing until it has passed required testing.
  • Expected: Patient care is transferred to another verified defibrillator.
  • Why it matters: A RAM test failure in the therapy or pacer processor path may affect therapy delivery reliability.

Verify the Reported Error and Behavior

  • Confirm the exact error shown or logged is PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST.
  • Confirm the therapy service code is a212.
  • Review the service log and note whether the error occurred during startup, during a self-test, during a therapy check, during pacing setup, or intermittently during use.
  • Expected: The error is confirmed in the service log or reproduced during startup or testing.
  • Why it matters: This confirms a true system-reported processor or RAM fault instead of a general staff-reported failure.

Confirm Device Status and Remove From Service

  • Check whether the device shows a service indicator, failed readiness condition, failed user test, or other therapy-related warning.
  • If the error is active or recorded as unresolved, label the unit Out of Service.
  • Expected: The device is not returned to clinical use while the processor RAM fault remains unresolved.
  • Why it matters: A defibrillator with a therapy processor memory failure should not be trusted for emergency use.

Check Basic External Conditions First

  • Verify the device is connected to a known-good power source or has a charged battery installed.
  • Inspect batteries, battery contacts, and external power connections for obvious damage, looseness, contamination, or poor seating.
  • Expected: Power source and battery condition are stable.
  • Why it matters: Unstable power can contribute to failed startup checks or abnormal processor behavior.

Perform a Controlled Power Cycle

  • Remove the device from patient care areas if appropriate.
  • Power the LIFEPAK 15 off, wait briefly, then restart the unit using a known-good battery or verified external power setup.
  • Recheck for the error or service indication.
  • Expected: The unit completes startup without the PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST error.
  • If resolved: Continue with required performance verification before return to service.
  • Why it matters: The stated response includes resetting the pacer processor, and a controlled restart may clear a transient processor fault.

Process and Review the Service Log

  • Access and process the service log according to department procedure.
  • Document PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST.
  • Document therapy service code a212.
  • Document the date and time of occurrence.
  • Document whether the error was active or historical.
  • Document whether it returned after restart.
  • Document any other therapy service codes present.
  • Expected: The service log confirms whether this was isolated or persistent.
  • Why it matters: Repeated RAM test failures strongly suggest a device-level fault requiring corrective action.

Run Required Post-Error Verification

  • Perform the required operational and performance checks used by your facility for LIFEPAK 15 return-to-service evaluation.
  • Confirm that the device completes its required checks and that PIP passes before considering return to service.
  • Expected: The device passes required testing without recurring PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST.
  • If resolved: Document the cleared condition and return the unit to service only after all required checks pass.
  • Why it matters: A cleared display alone is not enough for a defibrillator with a therapy processor error.

Check for Recurrence

  • Restart the device again and observe whether the error returns.
  • Review the service log after testing to confirm no new PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST entry was generated.
  • Expected: No recurrence of therapy service code a212.
  • Why it matters: Intermittent processor or RAM errors can appear cleared temporarily but return during self-test or therapy verification.

If the Problem Persists

If PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST or therapy service code a212 returns after restart, service log processing, and verification testing, external and simple causes have been ruled out.

A persistent RAM test failure indicates an internal therapy or pacer processor fault condition. Do not continue bedside troubleshooting.

The device should be removed from service, labeled Out of Service, and sent for repair or bench evaluation.

Do not return the LIFEPAK 15 to service until the corrective action path is completed and PIP passes.

Knowing when to stop is proper troubleshooting.

Clinical Use Tip

Never troubleshoot this error on an active patient. Move the patient to a backup device first, transfer monitoring and therapy readiness to another verified defibrillator, and maintain therapy continuity before continuing. A defibrillator with an unresolved therapy processor memory error should not be relied on for emergency response.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"LIFEPAK 15 reported PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST, therapy service code a212. Service information indicated RAM test failed and pacer processor reset was required. Device removed from service due to therapy processor fault concern."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Service log confirmed PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST. Power source and battery seating were checked. Error persisted after controlled restart and service log review, indicating likely internal therapy or pacer processor fault."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Processed service log, removed device from service, labeled unit Out of Service, and sent for corrective repair or bench evaluation. Device not returned to service pending corrective action and successful PIP completion."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Alarm behavior or error behavior, including whether PA_ERROR_RAM_TEST appeared during startup, self-test, therapy check, or intermittent use
  • Accessories swapped, including batteries, battery positions, therapy cable, electrodes, or test load used during controlled testing
  • Power behavior, including battery used during testing, external power source tested, and whether startup was normal
  • Environmental factors, including where the device was being stored or used when the error occurred
  • Indicator lights, including whether the service indicator, failed readiness indicator, or other warning was active
  • Final device status, including whether the error cleared, returned, required repair, or passed PIP before return to service

Final Thought

A RAM test failure in the LIFEPAK 15 therapy or pacer processor path is not a routine user-correctable issue. Clinical Engineering should confirm the error, rule out simple power-related conditions, process the service log, and escalate appropriately if the error persists. Patient safety and clear documentation are more important than forcing a device back into service.

That is successful troubleshooting.
