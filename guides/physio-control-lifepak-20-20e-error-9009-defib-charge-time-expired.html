Physio-Control LIFEPAK 20 / 20e

Error: 9009 – Defib Charge Time Expired

Asset Type

Defibrillator

Manufacturer

Physio-Control

Model

LIFEPAK 20 / 20e

What This Guide Helps With

Troubleshooting Error 9009 when LIFEPAK 20 / 20e defibrillator charging fails to complete within the allowed interval, potentially preventing timely therapy delivery.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

1. Ensure Patient Safety First

Remove the LIFEPAK 20 / 20e from patient use immediately if Error 9009 is active, recurring, or associated with delayed or failed charging.

Provide clinical staff with another verified defibrillator before troubleshooting continues.

  • Expected: Patient care is transferred to a known-good device.
  • Why it matters: A defibrillator that cannot reliably complete charging may not deliver therapy when needed.

2. Verify the Reported Error and Behavior

Confirm the exact Service Log error is:

  • 9009 – Defib Charge Time Expired

Determine when the condition occurs:

  • During startup or inspection
  • During manual defibrillator charging
  • During controlled therapy testing
  • Intermittently after repeated charge attempts

Observe associated behavior, such as:

  • Charge cycle does not complete
  • Charging takes abnormally long
  • Charge attempt terminates
  • Therapy readiness is affected
  • Expected: Error 9009 is reproducible or clearly documented in the Service Log.
  • Why it matters: Confirms a true system-reported charging timeout rather than an operator or test-setup issue.

3. Check the External Power Source

Verify the device is connected to a known-good AC outlet when applicable.

Check for:

  • Loose AC connection
  • Damaged power cord
  • Failed or switched-off power strip
  • Abnormal AC power indications

Test the outlet with a known-good method according to Clinical Engineering practice.

  • Expected: Stable external power is available and the device powers normally.
  • Why it matters: External power problems should be ruled out before attributing the timeout to the device.

4. Inspect External Cables and Physical Condition

Inspect the device for:

  • Loose external connections
  • Damaged therapy cables
  • Bent or contaminated connectors
  • Evidence of impact or liquid ingress
  • Unusual heat, odor, or sound

Do not continue charging tests if there are signs of overheating, burning odor, or significant physical damage.

  • Expected: No external damage or connection problem explains the charging failure.

5. Verify the Controlled Test Setup

During bench evaluation, confirm the therapy test configuration is correct and approved for the inspection being performed.

Check that:

  • The appropriate test load or analyzer is being used
  • Therapy accessories are fully seated
  • The selected operating mode is correct
  • The test sequence is not being interrupted prematurely
  • Expected: The charging timeout remains after setup errors are excluded.
  • Why it matters: An incorrect test configuration can create misleading therapy-related symptoms.

6. Evaluate Battery Condition

Error 9009 indicates that the defibrillator charging interval expired before charging completed. The manual corrective action identifies the A07 Battery as the required replacement path.

Before replacement, verify any readily observable battery-related findings, including:

  • Battery connection is secure
  • Battery-related status indications are abnormal
  • Charge-time failure is repeatable under controlled testing

If Error 9009 is confirmed, replace the A07 Battery according to approved service procedures.

  • Expected: The error no longer recurs and defibrillator charging completes normally.
  • Why it matters: The manufacturer corrective action specifically directs replacement of the A07 Battery for this error.

7. Perform Required Functional and Therapy Inspection

After battery replacement, complete the required functional and therapy inspection before returning the device to service.

Verify at minimum through the approved inspection process that:

  • Charging completes normally
  • Charge time is acceptable
  • Therapy functions operate correctly
  • No related Service Log error recurs
  • Required inspection criteria pass
  • Expected: The LIFEPAK 20 / 20e completes required testing without Error 9009.

8. Stop When Resolved

If replacement of the A07 Battery corrects the condition and all required functional and therapy inspections pass:

  • Document the repair
  • Confirm final device status
  • Return the LIFEPAK 20 / 20e to service

Do not continue unnecessary troubleshooting once the fault is corrected and required testing is successfully completed.

If the Problem Persists

If Error 9009 remains after patient safety has been addressed, external power has been verified, external connections and physical condition have been checked, controlled test setup has been confirmed, the A07 Battery has been replaced, and required inspection has been attempted, then common external causes and the specified battery corrective action have been exhausted.

A persistent charging timeout strongly indicates a device-level therapy or power-system problem requiring further qualified service evaluation.

The device should be:

  • Removed from service
  • Labeled Out of Service
  • Sent for repair or bench evaluation under approved service procedures

Do not return the device to clinical use until the charging problem is corrected and required functional and therapy testing passes.

Knowing when to stop is proper troubleshooting.

Clinical Use Tip

Never troubleshoot on an active patient. Move the patient to a backup device first and maintain therapy continuity. For a recurring defibrillator charge-time error, transfer therapy capability to another verified defibrillator before troubleshooting begins.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"LIFEPAK 20 / 20e reported Service Log Error 9009, Defib Charge Time Expired. Defibrillator charging failed to complete within the allowed interval during evaluation."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Confirmed recurring Error 9009 after external power, connections, physical condition, and controlled test setup were checked. A07 Battery identified by manufacturer corrective action as the required replacement path."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed device from service, replaced A07 Battery using approved service procedures, and completed required functional and therapy inspection. Charge cycle completed normally with no recurrence of Error 9009. Device returned to service after passing inspection."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Alarm behavior, including when Error 9009 occurred and whether it was reproducible
  • Accessories swapped, including therapy cables or other approved test accessories
  • Power behavior, including AC outlet testing, power cord condition, and battery status indications
  • Environmental factors, including evidence of impact, liquid ingress, unusual heat, odor, or sound
  • Indicator lights and device status indications observed during the failure
  • Test load or analyzer used
  • Charge behavior and approximate timing
  • Whether A07 Battery replacement was performed
  • Service Log behavior after repair
  • Functional and therapy inspection results
  • Final device status

Final Thought

Error 9009 represents a real defibrillator charging failure condition, not simply a nuisance log entry. Protect the patient first, rule out external causes, follow the specified A07 Battery corrective action, and verify therapy performance before release. Use a logical stopping point, escalate unresolved faults appropriately, and preserve clear CCR documentation for safe return to service or further repair.

That is successful troubleshooting.
