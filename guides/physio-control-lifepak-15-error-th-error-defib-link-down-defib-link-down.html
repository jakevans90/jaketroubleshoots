Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15

Error: TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN Defib Link Down

Asset Type

Defibrillator

Manufacturer

Physio-Control

Model

LIFEPAK 15

What This Guide Helps With

This guide helps troubleshoot TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN on a Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15 defibrillator when defib therapy is unavailable due to lost processor communication. This is a high-priority therapy fault because the unit may no longer be able to administer defibrillation therapy, synchronized cardioversion, or emergency shock delivery.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

Ensure Patient Safety First

  • Immediately remove the LIFEPAK 15 from patient use if this error is active or was reported during use.
  • Do not rely on this unit for defibrillation therapy, synchronized cardioversion, or emergency standby until the issue is corrected and verified.
  • Provide clinical staff with another verified defibrillator before continuing troubleshooting.
  • Expected: Patient care is transferred to another verified defibrillator.

Verify the Reported Error and Behavior

  • Power on the LIFEPAK 15 in a controlled, non-patient setting.
  • Confirm whether TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN or therapy service code a002 is present in the service log or appears during startup or operation.
  • Document when the error occurs, such as at startup, during self-test, when entering defib mode, during a charge attempt, or intermittently during use.
  • Confirm whether defib therapy functions are unavailable, disabled, or unable to proceed.
  • Expected: The error is reproducible or clearly observed.

Check Basic Power Condition

  • Verify the LIFEPAK 15 has a known-good battery installed.
  • Connect the unit to a known-good AC power adapter or power source if available.
  • Inspect the battery contacts, battery seating, and power accessories for obvious damage, contamination, or looseness.
  • Expected: The unit powers on normally without power interruptions.

Inspect External Physical Condition

  • Check the LIFEPAK 15 housing for signs of impact, fluid intrusion, cracked casing, damaged controls, or evidence the unit was dropped.
  • Inspect therapy cable ports, external connectors, and accessory connections for bent pins, debris, corrosion, or contamination.
  • Document any external damage that may support repair escalation.
  • Expected: No obvious external damage or contamination is found.

Remove Nonessential Accessories

  • Disconnect nonessential external accessories such as monitoring cables, sensors, and communication accessories.
  • Leave only what is needed for safe bench evaluation.
  • Restart the unit and check whether the same therapy service code remains.
  • Expected: TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN remains unchanged if the issue is internal to the device.

Check Therapy Cable and Electrode Setup Only as a Basic External Check

  • Inspect the therapy cable, paddles, or electrode connection for obvious damage.
  • Do not attempt clinical therapy or repeated charge/discharge troubleshooting on an active patient.
  • If your facility has a proper defib analyzer or test load and authorized test process, verify only enough to confirm that defib therapy is unavailable or inhibited.
  • Expected: The error remains associated with the device, not a damaged external therapy cable alone.

Review the Service Log

  • Access and process the service log according to facility and service procedures.
  • Record service code a002, the exact error name, date and time if available, and any repeated occurrences.
  • Look for related therapy, processor, communication, charging, or defib hardware service codes.
  • Expected: The service log supports a therapy processor communication failure.

Power Cycle Only as a Verification Step

  • Perform a controlled power cycle after documenting the error.
  • Recheck whether the service code returns.
  • Do not consider the device repaired simply because the error temporarily clears.
  • Expected: If the error returns, the fault is persistent. If it clears, the unit still requires proper service evaluation and performance verification before use.

Determine Whether the Issue Is External or Device-Related

  • Confirm that external checks have addressed the power source, battery seating, external damage, therapy cable condition, nonessential accessories, and service log evidence.
  • If TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN or service code a002 remains present or documented, treat the issue as device-related.
  • Expected: The failure is isolated to the LIFEPAK 15, not a simple accessory or setup condition.

Stop When Resolved

  • If an external issue is clearly found and corrected, complete required defibrillator functional testing and facility return-to-service checks before releasing the unit.
  • Do not return the LIFEPAK 15 to service unless defib therapy operation is verified through the required performance inspection.
  • Expected: The device passes required testing before clinical use.

If the Problem Persists

If TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN or therapy service code a002 persists after external checks, remove the LIFEPAK 15 from service. External causes such as power source, battery seating, visible damage, therapy cable condition, and nonessential accessories have been ruled out.

This condition should be treated as a device-related therapy communication failure requiring repair or authorized bench evaluation.

  • Remove the device from service.
  • Label the device Out of Service.
  • Send the device for repair or authorized bench evaluation.
  • Do not return the device to clinical use until corrective action is completed and required performance inspection passes.

Clinical Use Tip

Never troubleshoot this error on an active patient. Move the patient to a backup device first and maintain therapy continuity. A defibrillator with unresolved therapy processor communication errors should not remain in clinical standby.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"LIFEPAK 15 reported TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN / therapy service code a002. Staff reported defib therapy was unavailable or unable to proceed."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"External power, battery seating, therapy cable condition, visible damage, and nonessential accessories were checked. Error/service log supported internal defib processor communication failure."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed device from service and labeled Out of Service. Processed service log and escalated for repair. Device not returned to clinical use pending corrective action and required defibrillator performance inspection."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Exact error behavior and whether TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN appeared on screen or in the service log
  • Accessories swapped or inspected, including therapy cable, paddles, electrodes, sensors, and communication accessories
  • Power behavior, battery condition, battery seating, and AC adapter or power source used
  • Environmental factors such as impact damage, fluid exposure, contamination, storage location, or use conditions
  • Indicator lights, service indicators, service codes, related therapy faults, or startup messages
  • Final device status, including Out of Service label, repair escalation, and performance inspection status before return to service

Final Thought

TH_ERROR_DEFIB_LINK_DOWN is a high-priority defibrillator failure because the reported condition directly affects therapy delivery. Clinical Engineering troubleshooting should confirm the error, rule out simple external issues, document the service-code evidence, and escalate appropriately without attempting to keep the unit in service. Accurate CCR documentation protects patient safety and supports the repair path.

That is successful troubleshooting.
