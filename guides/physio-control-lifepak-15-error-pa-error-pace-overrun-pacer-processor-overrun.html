Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15

Error: PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN Pacer Processor Overrun

Asset Type

Defibrillator

Manufacturer

Physio-Control

Model

LIFEPAK 15

What This Guide Helps With

This guide helps troubleshoot PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN on a Physio-Control LIFEPAK 15 defibrillator. This error is associated with a pacer processor fault where the pacing pulse process may overrun and pacing current may be set to zero as a protective response.

Because this condition involves pacing output control, the device should not be trusted for clinical pacing until the error is evaluated, external causes are ruled out, and required service verification is completed.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

Ensure Patient Safety First

  • Immediately confirm whether the LIFEPAK 15 is connected to a patient or being used for pacing, monitoring, or defibrillation standby.
  • If the device is in active use, have clinical staff transition the patient to another verified defibrillator/monitor before troubleshooting.
  • Do not attempt to clear or ignore a therapy-related pacing fault during patient care.
  • Expected: Patient care continues on a safe, functional device.

Verify the Reported Error and Behavior

  • Confirm the exact displayed or logged error is PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN.
  • Review the service log and confirm therapy service code a20d if available.
  • Note whether the error appears during startup, when pacing mode is selected, during pacing output, during therapy checks, or after a power cycle.
  • Expected: The error is reproducible or clearly documented in the device service history.

Translate the Error Into the Real-World Failure

  • Treat PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN as a pacer processor/service-code fault.
  • The reported condition means the pacing pulse process overran and the device may set pacing current to zero as a protective response.
  • Expected: The device may be unable to deliver reliable pacing output.

Check Basic External Conditions

  • Confirm the battery is properly seated and has adequate charge.
  • Verify the AC power adapter, if used, is properly connected.
  • Confirm the therapy cable is fully connected.
  • Confirm QUIK-COMBO therapy electrodes or an appropriate test load are properly attached during controlled testing.
  • Inspect the device for visible damage, contamination, corrosion, or fluid intrusion.
  • Expected: No basic external condition explains the error.

Inspect Therapy Cable and Pacing Accessories

  • Inspect the therapy cable for bent or damaged pins.
  • Check for a cracked connector body, loose locking connection, cuts, kinks, exposed conductors, contamination, or residue.
  • If available, connect a known-good compatible therapy cable and appropriate test load during controlled evaluation.
  • Expected: The error remains unchanged if the fault is internal.

Power Cycle Only as a Confirmation Step

  • Power the device off and remove it from clinical availability.
  • Restart the unit using a known-good battery or verified power source.
  • Recheck whether PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN returns.
  • Expected: If the error returns, treat it as a persistent therapy service-code fault.

Do Not Attempt Clinical Pacing Use

  • Do not return the LIFEPAK 15 to patient use based only on a normal screen appearance after restart.
  • Do not leave the unit available in an emergency area if the error has not been fully evaluated.
  • Expected: The unit remains controlled by Clinical Engineering until repair evaluation is completed.

Remove From Service

  • If PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN or therapy service code a20d is confirmed, remove the device from service.
  • Apply an Out of Service label.
  • Document that the failure is related to the pacer processor/service-code pathway.
  • Expected: The device is no longer available for clinical use.

Send for Bench Evaluation or Repair

  • Forward the LIFEPAK 15 for service evaluation according to facility process.
  • Include the exact error, service code, when it occurred, and any external checks already completed.
  • Expected: Repair personnel receive enough detail to evaluate the pacing subsystem and verify safe operation before return to use.

Verify Before Return to Service

  • Confirm repair or service actions are completed before the device is returned to clinical availability.
  • Verify that required performance checks pass, including pacing output verification per facility and manufacturer service requirements.
  • Expected: No active service code remains, and pacer function is verified before the unit is placed back into service.

If the Problem Persists

If PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN returns after basic external checks, power verification, cable inspection, accessory review, and restart, external causes have been ruled out.

A persistent a20d therapy service code strongly indicates an internal pacing processor/service fault.

The device should be removed from service, labeled Out of Service, sent for repair or bench evaluation, and verified for pacing performance before return to clinical use.

Knowing when to stop is proper troubleshooting.

Clinical Use Tip

Never troubleshoot this error on an active patient. If pacing may be needed, transfer monitoring and therapy readiness to another verified defibrillator immediately. A LIFEPAK 15 with an unresolved pacing processor error should not be relied on for transcutaneous pacing.

Move the patient to a backup device first and maintain therapy continuity before any equipment evaluation begins.

Work Order Documentation (CCR Method)

CCR = Complaint, Cause, Resolution

Complaint

What was reported by the clinical staff.

Example:
"User reported LIFEPAK 15 displayed PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN with therapy service code a20d. Error indicates pacing pulse process overrun and pacing current set to zero."

Cause

What was observed during troubleshooting.

Example:
"Confirmed therapy-related pacer processor/service-code fault. External power, battery seating, therapy cable connection, and visible accessory condition checked with no external correction identified."

Resolution

What action was taken.

Example:
"Removed LIFEPAK 15 from service and labeled Out of Service. Sent for repair/bench evaluation due to persistent pacing processor fault. Pacer performance verification required before return to clinical use."

Helpful Details to Include (If Known)

  • Alarm behavior, including whether the error occurred at startup, during pacing mode, during pacing output, or after restart.
  • Accessories swapped, including therapy cable, QUIK-COMBO electrodes, or test load used.
  • Power behavior, including battery charge, battery seating, AC adapter status, and whether the error returned after a power cycle.
  • Environmental factors, including visible damage, contamination, fluid exposure, corrosion, abnormal heat, odor, or shutdowns.
  • Indicator lights, service LED status, audible alarms, displayed messages, and service log details.
  • Final device status, such as removed from service, labeled Out of Service, sent for repair, or returned only after verification.

Final Thought

PA_ERROR_PACE_OVERRUN should be treated as a serious therapy-related fault because it involves pacing pulse processing and current output control. Clinical Engineering should confirm the reported error, rule out simple external issues, remove the device from service, and escalate appropriately. Clear CCR documentation protects patient safety and helps repair teams act quickly.

That is successful troubleshooting.
