Asset Type

Infusion Pump

Manufacturer

Eitan Medical

Model

Sapphire

Models Covered

Eitan Medical Sapphire multi-therapy infusion pump covered by the cited USA User Manual, software version 15.0. Confirm the exact model, region, software revision, therapy configuration, administration set, power supply, cradle, bolus handle, lockbox, communication accessory, and labeling before service. This procedure does not automatically cover SapphireH100, Sapphire epidural-specific testing, or another Eitan pump.

Manual Reference

Eitan Medical Sapphire Infusion Pump User Manual, USA, software version 15.0, including Chapter 11 “Maintenance and Storage” and Chapter 12 “Technical Specifications”; Eitan Medical Sapphire FAQ; and Eitan FasTest PM Help. Use current serial-, software-, therapy-, accessory-, and region-specific controlled documentation. The current FasTest software and authorized service instructions govern certification limits, calibration decisions, service access, and certificate generation.

Manufacturer Recommendation / AEM Status

  • At least annually: The user manual requires the pump to be checked by an authorized service provider for yearly certification. Eitan recommends annual manual alarm testing, which may be included in certification.
  • After each use and as required: Inspect the housing and the entire power cord and plug; check battery charge and recharge as needed. Perform a preliminary mechanical-damage inspection before use.
  • Between patients: Clean and then disinfect the pump and all components under the facility protocol. For repeated use by one patient, clean and disinfect when visibly soiled, once weekly, or after home storage, whichever occurs first.
  • Battery: Test at least annually. Replace every 2 years or 500 charging cycles, whichever comes first. Recharge a stored battery at least every 12 months.
  • After a drop, damage, crack, or dent: Remove the pump from use and return it to the local representative for inspection.

Service boundary: Annual certification may be completed in-house only by appropriately trained personnel using the registered Eitan FasTest PM kit/software, or by an Eitan-approved service provider. Calibration, internal repair, epidural alarm protocols, and other controlled service tasks require the current authorized instructions and applicable service level. Do not substitute this public procedure for FasTest or the service manual.

If the facility changes the OEM interval or task set, manage the change through its approved AEM program, documented risk assessment, service history, and applicable regulatory requirements.

What This PM Procedure Covers

  • Identity, configuration, service history, field actions, annual certification status, and battery age/cycle status
  • Cleaning and inspection of the housing, safety door, pumping mechanism, sensors, display, controls, labels, connectors, power supply, cradle, and configured accessories
  • Startup, display, controls, speaker, LEDs, door/set sensing, bolus handle, external power, charging, and battery operation
  • Annual air-in-line, upstream/downstream occlusion, flow-accuracy, and alarm verification through the current FasTest or authorized procedure
  • Electrical-safety documentation, final configuration restoration, CMMS results, and return-to-service disposition

Required Tools and Equipment

  • Current Sapphire User Manual, controlled service manual/FasTest instructions, service bulletins, field actions, and facility policy
  • Eitan FasTest PM kit, part number 16024-000-1001, registered current software, and required Eitan-approved cables/adapters for qualified annual certification
  • New correct Sapphire administration set, compatible fluid container, clean water, clamps, and test tubing
  • Calibrated flow/volume measurement equipment and pressure measurement equipment only when required by the controlled FasTest procedure
  • Approved Sapphire power supply, mini cradle, rechargeable battery, bolus handle, lockbox, pole clamp, communication accessories, and other items in the maintained configuration
  • Approved cleaning/disinfection materials: Dispatch ready-to-use towels, Virex II 256, Virox AHP 5 RTU, Klor De, or 70% isopropyl alcohol as applicable; 70% IPA only for accessory electrical connectors
  • Electrical-safety analyzer suitable for the configured power supply and Type BF applied-part evaluation under facility policy
  • Inspection light, stopwatch, PPE, dry lint-free cloths, foam swabs where permitted, and CMMS worksheet

Safety: Disconnect the pump from the patient before any test. Turn it off and disconnect all sets, power, and accessories before cleaning. Never immerse, spray, steam-autoclave, ethylene-oxide sterilize, or allow fluid into the housing, speaker holes, battery chamber, connector pins, or pores. The pump must be completely dry before power is connected.

Step-by-Step PM Procedure

1. Remove From Clinical Use

Action: Confirm the pump is not connected to a patient, stop therapy, remove medication and the patient-use set, protect patient information, label the pump unavailable, and complete infection-control preparation.

Expected outcome: The pump is isolated and safe for bench testing.

2. Verify Identity, Configuration, and Certification Status

Action: Record the asset tag, Sapphire model, serial number, software revision, enabled therapies, region/configuration, administration-set type, battery, power supply, cradle, bolus handle, lockbox, communication accessories, and labels. Review the last certification date, FasTest certificate, battery replacement date, and charge-cycle information when available.

Expected outcome: The exact maintained configuration and all due work are identified.

3. Review History and Field Actions

Action: Review prior PM/certification, repairs, drops, fluid exposure, delivery complaints, nuisance or missed alarms, battery/runtime issues, error history, software/configuration changes, recalls, service bulletins, and field actions.

Expected outcome: Recurring faults and required corrective actions are known before testing.

4. Clean and Disinfect

Action: With the pump off and disconnected, use only a manual-approved agent at its correct dilution. Apply solution to a cloth or sponge and squeeze it so it does not drip. Wipe exterior surfaces in vertical and horizontal passes with complete coverage. Follow the manual's cleaning or disinfection cycle and contact/drying instructions. Use only a damp cloth on sensor fingertips and 70% IPA on accessory electrical connectors; allow connectors to air-dry at least 3 minutes. Dry the pump completely before power is applied.

Expected outcome: The pump and accessories are clean, disinfected as required, dry, and free of residue or fluid entry.

5. Inspect the Housing, Door, and Pump Mechanism

Action: Inspect all enclosure surfaces for cracks, dents, separation, loose parts, fluid entry, and impact damage. Inspect the safety door, hinge, latch/locking tooth, anchor, internal white panel, pumping mechanism, sensor fingers, bubble detector, cassette path, and P-to-C/power-communication connector for damage, contamination, wear, and free movement.

Expected outcome: The enclosure and fluid-path interface are intact, clean, secure, and mechanically functional.

6. Inspect Controls, Labels, Power, and Accessories

Action: Verify the touchscreen/LCD, keypad, LEDs, speaker openings, labels, power socket, cords, plugs, mini cradle, pole clamp, bolus handle, lockbox, communication accessories, and all connector pins/contacts. Check the entire power cord and plug for damage.

Expected outcome: Controls, identification, power components, mounting hardware, and accessories are complete and undamaged.

7. Inspect and Evaluate the Battery

Action: Inspect the technician-replaceable Sapphire Li-ion battery and compartment for damage, swelling, corrosion, odor, overheating evidence, contamination, or poor contact. Review battery age and charging cycles. Test battery condition at least annually through the authorized process; verify the two-year/500-cycle replacement interval and recharge requirements after storage.

Expected outcome: The correct battery is safe, within its service interval, accepts charge, and meets the current controlled test criteria.

8. Verify Startup and User Interface

Action: Connect the approved power supply and verify the yellow Charge LED behavior. Power on and observe startup, display, touchscreen/keys, indicators, speaker, date/time, battery icon, power-source indication, menus, and configured therapy modes. Do not alter clinical configuration without authorization.

Expected outcome: Startup completes without error and the interface, indications, sounder, power recognition, and approved configuration operate correctly.

9. Verify Cassette, Door, and Accessory Recognition

Action: Using a new correct Sapphire administration set, verify proper cassette placement, door closure/latching, set/door sensing, and safe removal. Exercise the bolus handle and other configured accessories under the authorized test workflow.

Expected outcome: The set loads correctly; the door latches securely; incorrect/open conditions are detected; and configured accessories are recognized and functional.

10. Complete Annual FasTest Setup and Inspection

Action: A qualified technician connects the registered current FasTest PM kit/software, identifies the pump, completes the software-directed visual, mechanical, and electrical inspection, and verifies speaker, LEDs, door sensor, bolus handle, and other prompted functions. Record kit/software revision and calibrated test-equipment identifiers.

Expected outcome: The correct pump and test setup are recognized, all prompted inspections pass, and traceability is established.

11. Verify Air-in-Line Detection

Action: Complete the FasTest air-detector verification exactly as prompted. If using the public manual's annual alarm check, confirm air detection is enabled, connect a new set without a fluid container, start at 100 mL/h, and verify the Air in Line alarm while disconnected from any patient.

Expected outcome: Air detection and alarm annunciation pass the current authorized acceptance criteria.

12. Verify Upstream and Downstream Occlusion

Action: Complete the FasTest occlusion-sensor verification. For the public manual alarm check, run 600 mL/h over 5 minutes and close the upstream clamp to verify Upstream Occlusion; repeat by clamping or pinching downstream to verify Downstream Occlusion. Stand 1 meter away and verify the alarm is visible and audible.

Expected outcome: Upstream and downstream occlusions are detected and annunciated, and the calibrated sensor results meet the current authorized criteria.

13. Verify Flow Accuracy

Action: Complete the FasTest flow-accuracy stage using the specified new set, setup geometry, fluid, programmed rate/volume, measurement device, stabilization, and software prompts. Do not replace the FasTest acceptance limits with the general technical specification. For reference only, the user manual states ±2.5% under defined normal conditions.

Expected outcome: Measured delivery passes the current FasTest criteria and the result is captured on the certification record.

14. Complete Certification and Controlled Service

Action: Resolve every FasTest failure under the authorized service level. Perform calibration, internal repair, software update, or epidural-specific testing only with the required training, current controlled instructions, and Eitan authorization. Generate and retain the manufacturer-supported certification result.

Expected outcome: Annual certification is complete, no unauthorized service was performed, and all failures are corrected or the pump is quarantined.

15. Perform Electrical Safety Testing

Action: Test the configured pump, approved external power supply, cradle, and accessories under the current controlled procedure, equipment labeling, facility policy, and applicable standard. Document the equipment class, Type BF applied-part classification, test method, limits, analyzer, and results.

Expected outcome: All applicable electrical-safety measurements pass their governing limits.

16. Restore, Finalize, and Document

Action: Remove test sets and prescriptions, restore the authorized configuration and alarm settings, verify date/time and accessories, complete a final startup/functional check, clean and dry the equipment, and document all measurements, certificate details, battery status, parts, failures, and disposition.

Expected outcome: No test setup remains, records are traceable, and the pump is formally returned to service or removed from service.

Electrical Safety Testing

The Sapphire uses a Class II external power supply and has Type BF applied-part classification. The public user manual does not establish one universal PM leakage limit for every pump, supply, cradle, accessory, region, and test method. Use current labeling, controlled Eitan/FasTest instructions, the applicable electrical-safety standard, and facility policy.

Pass / Fail Criteria

Pass

  • Exact identity, software/configuration, accessories, field-action status, battery status, and annual certification due date are documented
  • Housing, door, mechanism, sensors, cassette path, controls, display, speaker, LEDs, labels, connectors, power equipment, mounting hardware, and accessories pass inspection
  • Startup, user interface, power/charge indications, door/set sensing, bolus handle, and configured functions operate correctly
  • Battery passes the annual controlled test and is within the 2-year/500-cycle replacement interval
  • Current FasTest or authorized annual certification passes air detection, occlusion sensing, flow accuracy, alarms, and every other prompted inspection
  • Electrical safety passes, the authorized configuration is restored, and no unresolved error, complaint, field action, or test setting remains

Remove From Service

  • Annual certification is overdue or the required qualified personnel, current FasTest/service instructions, registered software, or calibrated equipment are unavailable
  • The pump was dropped, is cracked/dented, has fluid entry, or has damage/contamination affecting the door, mechanism, sensors, controls, connectors, power system, mounting, or accessories
  • Any air-in-line, upstream/downstream occlusion, flow-accuracy, speaker, LED, door sensor, bolus handle, alarm, startup, control, power, battery, or electrical-safety test fails
  • The battery is unsafe, fails testing, or has reached 2 years/500 charging cycles without authorized replacement
  • Configuration cannot be verified/restored, a certification failure remains unresolved, or any condition could permit inaccurate infusion, free flow, missed detection, unavailable alarm, interruption, or electrical hazard

Copy/Paste CMMS Work Order Procedure

Do not return the Sapphire to service with overdue or failed annual certification, damage or fluid entry, an expired/failed battery, a failed delivery/sensor/alarm/power/electrical-safety result, an unresolved field action, incorrect configuration, or any temporary test setting remaining.
