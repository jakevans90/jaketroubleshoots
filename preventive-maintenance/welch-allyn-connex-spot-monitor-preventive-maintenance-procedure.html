Asset Type

Vital Signs Monitor

Manufacturer

Welch Allyn

Model

Connex Spot Monitor

Models Covered

Welch Allyn Connex Spot Monitor (CSM) 7100, 7300 Bluetooth, and 7500 Wi-Fi series covered by the cited service manual. Confirm the exact model, software, NIBP hose type, SpO2 technology, temperature option, power arrangement, stand/APM, wired/wireless interfaces, barcode scanner, and accessories before service. This procedure does not cover the Connex 6000 Series or another Welch Allyn monitor.

Manual Reference

Welch Allyn Connex Spot Monitor Service Manual, document 80019225; current Connex Spot Monitor Instructions for Use; and the official Connex Spot Monitor Service and Maintenance Toolset. The current manual and Welch Allyn Service Tool instructions govern software-specific prompts, calibration, repair, and return-to-service requirements.

Manufacturer Recommendation / AEM Status

  • Annually: Perform basic functional verification of the installed NIBP, SpO2, SureTemp Plus, and Braun ThermoScan PRO 6000 options. The current service-manual schedule recommends replacing the monitor battery annually or sooner when remaining capacity no longer meets workflow requirements.
  • Every 4 months: Clean the electrical contacts on an installed Braun ThermoScan PRO 6000 and its dock using 70% isopropyl alcohol.
  • Routine / facility schedule: Clean the monitor, stand, APM work surface, bins, cables, and accessories under facility protocol using approved agents. Perform the periodic checks in the current Instructions for Use.
  • After damage, malfunction, failed basic verification, case opening, or internal-part replacement other than the battery: Complete Gold-edition functional verification and calibration before return to service.

Service boundary: Basic annual verification can use the Standard unlicensed Welch Allyn Service Tool and the manual's procedures. Gold-edition calibration and complete verification require the applicable license and trained service personnel. No component-level circuit-board repair is supported. Service not described in the manual belongs to Baxter or an authorized service center.

If the facility changes the OEM interval or task set, manage the change through its approved AEM program, documented risk assessment, service history, and applicable regulatory requirements.

What This PM Procedure Covers

  • Identity, configuration, history, field actions, service-tool status, battery age, and accessories
  • Cleaning and inspection of the monitor, display, connectors, stand/APM, power equipment, cables, hoses, sensors, thermometer, and dock
  • Startup, display, touchscreen, LED, beeper, battery, power, charging, interfaces, and accessory checks
  • Annual NIBP leak, overpressure, and accuracy verification
  • Configuration-specific SpO2 and SureTemp Plus or Braun ThermoScan PRO 6000 verification
  • Gold Service Tool escalation, electrical-safety applicability, documentation, and disposition

Required Tools and Equipment

  • Current Connex Spot Monitor Service Manual, Instructions for Use, service bulletins, field actions, configuration records, and facility policy
  • PC with current Welch Allyn Service Tool; Standard unlicensed edition for basic checks or active Gold license for complete verification/calibration when required
  • Micro-USB service cable and approved power supply; service test box, Ethernet cable, and compatible wireless router when testing those interfaces
  • NIBP test-volume fixture, correct hoses/Y-tube and fittings, hand bulb/valve, and calibrated pressure meter reading to at least two decimal places with ±0.5 mmHg accuracy
  • Compatible simulator for the installed Nonin, Nellcor, or Masimo SpO2 technology; DOC-10 extension cable when required
  • SureTemp calibration key 06138-000 and Welch Allyn 9600 Plus Calibration Tester when testing the installed SureTemp system/probe
  • Welch Allyn 9600 Plus Calibration Tester and new probe covers when testing an installed Braun ThermoScan PRO 6000
  • Correct monitor battery, APM battery/power components, cuffs, hoses, sensors, probes, thermometer/dock, scanner, stand, and configured accessories
  • Manual-approved wipes or 70% isopropyl alcohol, cotton swabs, lint-free cloths, inspection light, PPE, and CMMS worksheet
  • Electrical-safety analyzer when testing a configuration or post-repair condition for which the service manual requires it

Safety: Never service a patient-connected monitor. Disconnect mains before cleaning. Do not immerse, autoclave, sterilize, or permit liquid entry. If liquid may have entered, remove the monitor from use until qualified personnel have dried, inspected, and tested it.

Step-by-Step PM Procedure

1. Remove From Clinical Use

Action: Confirm no patient is connected. Remove patient-use cuffs, probes, sensors, and identifying data as required; label the monitor unavailable and complete infection-control preparation.

Expected outcome: The monitor is isolated and safe for service.

2. Verify Identity and Configuration

Action: Record asset tag, model/series, serial number, software and firmware, NIBP hose type, SpO2 technology, temperature option, battery, external supply, stand/APM, network interfaces, scanner, and accessories. Match installed options to the model/configuration label and CMMS record.

Expected outcome: The exact configuration and required test branches are identified.

3. Review History, Due Work, and Field Actions

Action: Review prior PM, repairs, drops, liquid exposure, measurement complaints, intermittent alarms, battery/runtime issues, service-tool logs, firmware changes, recalls, service bulletins, and field actions. Determine whether Gold verification is mandatory.

Expected outcome: Recurring faults, annual tasks, and escalation requirements are known before testing.

4. Clean the Monitor and Accessories

Action: Power down and disconnect mains. Follow the cleaning-agent restrictions in the current manual and manufacturer instructions. Wipe the monitor, stand, APM, bins, cords, cables, hoses, and exposed surfaces until no soil remains; air-dry components and wipe the LCD dry. Clean patient accessories under their own instructions. Do not use bleach on metal electrical contacts.

Expected outcome: All maintained items are clean, dry, and free of residue or liquid entry.

5. Clean Braun Contacts When Applicable

Action: If equipped with Braun ThermoScan PRO 6000, slightly dampen a cotton swab with 70% IPA. Clean the thermometer and dock contacts separately, allowing each to air-dry for 1 minute before redocking. Complete this at least every 4 months.

Expected outcome: Contacts are clean, dry, undamaged, and support reliable data transfer.

6. Inspect the Monitor, Mounting, and Accessories

Action: Inspect housing, display/touchscreen, LED, speaker, battery cover, connectors, labels, feet, fasteners, seals, power supply/cord, stand/APM, work surface, bins, pole/wall mounting, cuffs, hoses, SpO2 cables/sensors, temperature probe/well or Braun thermometer/dock, scanner, and interface cables. Look for cracks, looseness, contamination, wear, exposed conductors, corrosion, overheating, or liquid entry.

Expected outcome: The monitor and every configured item are complete, secure, legible, clean, and undamaged.

7. Replace and Verify the Battery

Action: Review battery age and workflow/runtime history. Under the current schedule, replace the battery annually or earlier when capacity is inadequate. Inspect the compartment and contacts; install the correct battery and verify charging and battery operation. Evaluate an APM battery separately when fitted.

Expected outcome: The correct battery is within interval, charges normally, and supports expected operation without damage or warning.

8. Verify Startup and Basic Host Functions

Action: Power on using approved supply and battery. Verify POST, display, touchscreen alignment/response, LED, beeper, date/time, power and charge indications, battery status, menus, scanner, and configured wired/wireless interfaces. Preserve approved settings and patient-data policy.

Expected outcome: Startup completes without unresolved error and all host functions operate correctly.

9. Connect the Service Tool and Preserve Records

Action: Connect the correct service cable and launch the current Service Tool. Identify the exact device, installed modules, firmware, status, usage history, and active logs. Save required records and select the authorized test level.

Expected outcome: The correct monitor is recognized and traceable service records are available.

10. Perform the NIBP Leak Test

Action: Use the specified test volume, Y-tube, pressure meter, and Standard/Gold Service Tool workflow. The automated test begins at 250 ±10 mmHg and evaluates pressure loss over 15 seconds.

Expected outcome: Leak is no more than 5 mmHg in 15 seconds, and the service record shows Pass.

11. Perform NIBP Overpressure Verification

Action: Run the Service Tool overpressure test in Adult and Neonate modes. Confirm pump shutdown and valve opening within each specified range.

Expected outcome: Adult activates from 280–329 mmHg; Neonate activates from 130–164 mmHg.

12. Perform NIBP Accuracy Verification

Action: Connect the 500 cc volume, zero the calibrated meter, and compare monitor/Service Tool pressure at 0, 50, 150, and 250 mmHg after the required stabilization. Record both readings; verify valve release to zero. Use the manual's on-device pressure-verification alternative only with a supported software revision and exact setup.

Expected outcome: Difference is within ±1 mmHg at 0 and ±3 mmHg at 50, 150, and 250 mmHg.

13. Verify Installed SpO2 Technology

Action: Connect the compatible simulator and allow up to 30 seconds to stabilize. For Nonin/Nellcor, apply 90% saturation and 60 bpm. For Masimo, apply 80% saturation and 61 bpm.

Expected outcome: Nonin/Nellcor reads 90% ±1 sat point and 60 ±1 bpm. Masimo reads 80% ±3 sat points and 61 ±1 bpm.

14. Verify SureTemp Plus When Installed

Action: Use the calibration key to verify the system at 97.3 °F (36.3 °C). When the probe is included in the annual test, use Direct mode and the 9600 Plus tester at 96.8, 101.3, and 105.8 °F, allowing the tester and probe to stabilize as directed.

Expected outcome: Calibration-key result is 97.3 ±0.2 °F (36.3 ±0.1 °C); each full probe/system point is within ±0.2 °F (±0.1 °C).

15. Verify Braun ThermoScan PRO 6000 When Installed

Action: Inspect and clean the lens/sensor, acclimate thermometer and tester in the same room for at least 30 minutes, verify full charge, enter CAL mode, and test with new probe covers at the 9600 Plus low, medium, and high set points. Observe the required delay between readings.

Expected outcome: Each reading is within ±0.4 °F (±0.2 °C) of the tester set point and the thermometer communicates correctly with the dock.

16. Complete Gold Verification When Required

Action: If any basic result fails, or the device was damaged, malfunctioning, opened, or had an internal part replaced other than the battery, a qualified technician uses the Gold Service Tool to complete the full host/module verification and calibration. Confirm the final record contains every test required for the model.

Expected outcome: Complete verification/calibration passes, or the monitor remains removed from service.

17. Perform Configuration-Appropriate Electrical Safety

Action: Determine whether the unit uses only the certified external power brick or includes an internal-power APM/configuration. Follow the service manual and facility standard. After applicable open-case repair, document ground continuity and leakage; do not routinely HiPot the device.

Expected outcome: Required measurements pass and prohibited/unnecessary stress testing is avoided.

18. Restore, Finalize, and Document

Action: Remove test connections, restore authorized settings and alarm limits, verify date/time and interfaces, clear temporary test data, perform final startup and measurement sanity checks, clean/dry the equipment, and document results, equipment, software, battery, parts, Gold escalation, and disposition.

Expected outcome: Configuration is restored, records are traceable, and the monitor is formally returned to service or quarantined.

Electrical Safety Testing

Baxter recommends ground-continuity and leakage-current testing after open-case repairs where an internal mains power supply is present. For devices supplied only by the certified external medical power brick, the manual does not recommend additional after-service testing of that brick or downstream DC circuitry. Identify the exact power configuration before selecting a test.

When applicable, the manual specifies ground continuity from the equipotential stud to the IEC protective-earth pin of ≤0.1 Ω and leakage from the equipotential stud to mains line/neutral of <500 µA. Baxter does not recommend routine HiPot/dielectric-withstand testing. If insulation integrity is in doubt, such as after saline ingress or repeated RCD trips, return the device to Baxter.

Pass / Fail Criteria

Pass

  • Identity, configuration, accessories, history, field actions, annual due status, battery, and test level are documented
  • Monitor, housing, display, controls, labels, connectors, power equipment, mounting, cables, hoses, sensors, temperature option, and accessories pass inspection
  • Cleaning is complete; Braun contacts were cleaned within four months when applicable
  • Current battery replacement is documented and startup, host functions, power, charging, interfaces, LED, beeper, and controls operate correctly
  • NIBP leak, overpressure, and accuracy meet the exact service-manual limits
  • Installed SpO2 and temperature options meet their configuration-specific limits
  • Gold verification/calibration is complete whenever required; applicable electrical safety passes; settings are restored; no unresolved fault remains

Remove From Service

  • Required annual verification, battery replacement, qualified personnel, current instructions, service software, or calibrated equipment are unavailable
  • There is damage, liquid entry, contamination, missing safety parts, unsafe mounting, failed battery, exposed conductor, or defective accessory
  • Startup, touchscreen, display, LED, beeper, power, charging, interface, scanner, or Service Tool communication fails
  • Any NIBP, SpO2, SureTemp, Braun, or other installed measurement result is outside its stated limit
  • The device requires Gold verification/calibration and that work is incomplete or failed
  • Required electrical safety fails, configuration cannot be restored, or any unresolved condition could cause inaccurate measurement, missed alarm, data error, interruption, or electrical hazard

Copy/Paste CMMS Work Order Procedure

Do not return the Connex Spot Monitor to service with overdue annual verification or battery replacement, a failed measurement or host function, damage or liquid entry, incomplete required Gold verification, failed electrical safety, incorrect configuration, or any unresolved service condition.
