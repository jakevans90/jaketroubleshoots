Manual Reference

Hill-Rom CareAssist and CareAssist ES Bed Service Manual, Product No. P1170, MAN330 REV 4.

Manufacturer Recommendation / AEM Status

The manufacturer advises annual bed service. Batteries are replaced every 3 years and bed-exit tape switches every 5 years by facility-authorized maintenance personnel. The annual checklist covers overall condition, structure, welds, labels, hardware, headboard/footboard, AC cord, leakage current, pendant, columns, actuators, sleep deck, CPR, gas springs, articulation, battery, siderails, foot extension, pivot points, casters, fifth wheel, brakes/steering, accessories, and fitted scale, Bed Exit, SideCom, and SafeView features. The manual states that only facility-authorized personnel should service the bed.

Use the current serial- and configuration-specific service manual. If a facility performs this PM at a different interval or changes the listed activities, that should be handled under the facility’s approved AEM program, risk assessment, maintenance history, and applicable regulatory requirements.

What This PM Procedure Covers

  • Annual visual, mechanical, functional, and electrical-safety inspection
  • Three-year battery and five-year bed-exit tape-switch interval review
  • Bed motion, CPR, controls, siderails, brakes, steering, and alarm checks
  • Scale, Bed Exit, SideCom, SafeView, and fifth-wheel checks when fitted

Required Tools and Equipment

  • Current CareAssist service manual and normal service hand tools
  • Electrical safety/leakage-current analyzer
  • T25 Torx screwdriver and 250 lb (113 kg) of weights in 25 lb (11 kg) increments when scale calibration is applicable
  • Manufacturer-approved replacement parts and facility-approved cleaning supplies as needed

Step-by-Step PM Procedure

1. Verify Identity and Configuration

Action: Record asset number, CareAssist model, serial number, product number, revision, voltage, and fitted scale, Bed Exit, SideCom, SafeView, and fifth-wheel options.

Expected outcome: The correct requirements and manual apply.

2. Review History and Prepare the Bed

Action: Review prior failures and repairs. Remove the bed from clinical use, remove accessories/load as needed, unplug when directed, and follow facility infection-control and lockout practices.

Expected outcome: Known issues are addressed and service hazards controlled.

3. Inspect Overall Condition

Action: Inspect the bed structure, frame, base, welds, symmetry, corrosion, impact damage, paint, labels, and loose or missing hardware.

Expected outcome: The frame/base are not twisted and all safety-related components are intact and readable.

4. Inspect Headboard and Footboard

Action: Check condition, attachment, fit, and safe removal/reinstallation.

Expected outcome: Both boards are intact, secure, and safe.

5. Inspect the AC Power Cord and Plug

Action: Inspect the full cord, strain relief, and one-piece molded plug for cuts, cracking, loose blades, discoloration, overheating, arcing, or exposed conductors.

Expected outcome: No damage; replace a defective cord assembly.

6. Perform Leakage-Current Testing

Action: Connect the bed through the safety tester, confirm the bed is supplied by it, and measure without operating bed functions.

Expected outcome: Leakage current is less than 100 µA; investigate the cord and power supply if outside specification.

7. Inspect and Test the Patient Pendant

Action: Inspect the connector and cable, reconnect securely, and hold each button several seconds.

Expected outcome: Each button commands the correct function and motion remains continuous without intermittency.

8. Inspect and Test Hi-Low Columns

Action: Check column fasteners and retaining hardware, then fully raise and lower the bed. Verify the Bed Not Down indicator behavior.

Expected outcome: Smooth, quiet motion without friction or overload indication; indicator extinguishes in low position.

9. Inspect and Test the Head Section

Action: Inspect actuator pins and retaining clips, fully raise/lower the head section, and inspect the plastic sleep deck and drive system for binding or damage.

Expected outcome: Intact hardware and smooth, quiet motion.

10. Test CPR Releases and Gas Springs

Action: Inspect both CPR handles, cables, mechanism, screws, and gas-spring attachments/shafts. Test CPR from both sides and confirm the mechanism relocks with Head Up.

Expected outcome: Controlled lowering from high to intermediate and then low, no oil on shafts, and positive relock.

11. Inspect and Test Knee, Foot, and Automatic Contour

Action: Inspect actuator pins/retainers; fully exercise knee and foot sections. Confirm automatic contour raises the knee to mid-height with head elevation and lowers it as the head returns low.

Expected outcome: Correct coordinated motion without abnormal noise, friction, or overload.

12. Test Dining Chair and Trendelenburg Functions

Action: With functions unlocked, operate Dining Chair, return the deck flat, and test Trendelenburg and reverse Trendelenburg.

Expected outcome: All sections reach the commanded positions smoothly and quietly.

13. Check Battery Operation and Age

Action: Unplug the bed and operate all functions from caregiver controls. Inspect battery dates.

Expected outcome: Battery operation is normal; charge/replace as needed and replace batteries when over 3 years old.

14. Test Scale and Bed Exit, If Fitted

Action: Calibrate the scale using the exact manual procedure and listed weights. Function-test all Bed Exit modes/alarms and review the five-year tape-switch interval.

Expected outcome: Scale calibration completes, Bed Exit works properly, and due tape switches are replaced.

15. Inspect Siderails and Caregiver Controls

Action: Check head/foot siderails for bending or twisting, verify audible latch engagement, inspect covered fasteners and cable routing, and test caregiver controls.

Expected outcome: Siderails latch securely and cables/controls are undamaged and functional.

16. Inspect Foot Extension and Pivot Points

Action: Operate the foot extension through its positions and confirm locking when the lever is released. Exercise articulations and investigate noise; lubricate only as directed.

Expected outcome: Secure locking and quiet, unrestricted articulation.

17. Inspect Casters, Fifth Wheel, Brakes, and Steering

Action: Inspect tread/cuts/wear, confirm fifth-wheel motion if fitted, apply brakes and try to move the bed, then verify steering.

Expected outcome: Wheels are serviceable, brakes prevent movement, and steering works correctly.

18. Test Brake-Not-Set Alarm

Action: Connect AC power, place brakes in neutral, and confirm the alarm sounds; set the brakes and confirm it stops.

Expected outcome: Alarm behavior matches brake position.

19. Inspect Accessories and Optional Communication/Alerts

Action: Check fitted accessories. Inspect/test the SideCom junction box, features, cable, and connector pins and test SafeView alerts when fitted.

Expected outcome: Every installed feature is secure and functional.

20. Restore, Final-Test, and Document

Action: Correct defects with approved parts, restore covers/accessories, complete a final function/readiness check, label the bed, and document measurements, replacements, and disposition.

Expected outcome: The bed is complete and safe to return or clearly removed from service.

Pass / Fail Criteria

PM Pass Criteria

  • Annual checklist is complete
  • Structure, boards, cord, controls, motion, CPR, battery, siderails, foot extension, casters, brakes, steering, alarms, accessories, and fitted options pass
  • Leakage current is below 100 µA
  • Three- and five-year replacements are current

Remove From Service Criteria

  • Structural/weld damage or unsafe board, siderail, caster, brake, or steering condition
  • Damaged mains components or leakage current at or above 100 µA
  • Failed CPR, essential bed motion, battery backup, or safety alarm
  • Unresolved fitted-feature failure, overdue safety-related replacement, or incomplete authorized repair
